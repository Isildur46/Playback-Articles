# RITE 仪式

Rites, as intense as actions characterised by gestures and verbal forms, codified by tradition and which help to establish a communication between human and divine, are phenomena very similar to dramatic representations.

仪式，作为一种以手势和语言形式为特征、由传统所规范、并有助于建立人与神之间沟通的强烈行为，是一种与戏剧表演极为相似的现象。

My intent is to try and find a comparison between these two phenomena and then individuate eventual connections with the Playback Theatre.

我的目的是尝试找出这两种现象之间的共同点，进而发掘它们与一人一故事剧场之间的潜在联系。

The questions which I will try to give possible answers to are:

我将尝试回答以下问题：

- What is the meaning of rite according to an anthropological vision? . What connection exists between rite and theatre? 从人类学的视角来看，仪式的意义是什么？仪式与戏剧之间存在怎样的联系？
- In what position does Playback Theatre find itself? 一人一故事剧场处于怎样的位置？

To examine the significance of rite more closely, I will refer to various anthropological studies that have examined it in its different expressive forms depending on the different functions which are assumed in different contexts.

为了更深入地探讨仪式的意义，我将参考多项人类学研究，这些研究根据仪式在不同语境中承担的不同功能，对其各种表现形式进行了考察。

To analyse the connection between rite and theatre, I will substantially retrace the thoughts of Turner, who widely examined the subject within his theoretical studies. Finally, during the search of a placing for the Playback Theatre, I will rely on my personal reflections as well as the theoretical contribution of the founders and other practitioners who, in the past years, have collaborated and elaborated a vision of Playback Theatre.

为了分析仪式与戏剧之间的联系，我将重点追溯特纳（Turner）的理论思想，他在其理论研究中对这一主题进行了广泛探讨。最后，在探讨一人一故事剧场的定位时，我将结合个人思考，并参考其创始人及其他实践者的理论贡献——他们在过去几年中通力合作，共同构建了一人一故事剧场的理论框架。

# The rite 仪式

Rite, from the Latin word ritus which means custom or usage, is intended as a sequence of acts regulated by strictly codified rules. With time, different meaning have been attributed by various people interested in this subject, who have also ana lysed the concept from different points of view.

“仪式”（Rite）一词源自拉丁语“ritus”，意为习俗或惯例，指由严格编纂的规则所规范的一系列行为。随着时间的推移，关注这一主题的各界人士赋予了它不同的含义，并从不同角度对这一概念进行了分析。

## Supernatural control 超自然控制

Rites are seen as a way of “playing” with all that which goes beyond the sphere of earthly beings, to put oneself in connection with the divine world to then be able to overcome any fear, to find answers and to give form to projections. Concerning this, Malinowsky wrote in 1922 that the rite is an activity that allows man “to act in the supernatural world which exists due to his desires, his hopes, his fears and his expectations!!. (”Argonauts of Western Pacific!!)

仪式被视为一种“玩味”超越尘世存在范畴之事的方式，旨在使人与神界建立联系，从而克服一切恐惧、寻找答案并使想象具象化。关于这一点，马林诺夫斯基在1922年写道，仪式是一种活动，它使人类能够“在超自然世界中行动——这个世界正是由人类的欲望、希望、恐惧和期待所构成的！！”。（《西太平洋的阿耳戈英雄！！》）

## The control of natural forces 对自然力量的掌控

Man, in the course of history, has had to confront himself with natural events of which he may not have found an immediate explanation, therefore causing anxiety and fear. Rites are intended as a technique to dominate natural forces when there is insufficient technical and scientific knowledge. According to Fraser “magical and religious beliefs have the purpose of explaining natural phenomena, rites have the purpose of controlling them” (1911-1915).

在历史长河中，人类不得不面对那些无法立即解释的自然现象，这往往引发焦虑与恐惧。当技术和科学知识尚不充分时，仪式便成为驾驭自然力量的一种手段。据弗雷泽所言：“魔法和宗教信仰旨在解释自然现象，而仪式的目的则是控制这些现象”（1911-1915）。

## The need of protection 保护的需求

Rites are considered as a means of overcoming daily difficulties as those suggested by traditional patterns of behaviour. The ritual repetition is considered as an instrument to reduce anxiety, as it creates a feeling of security and a bond with the past.
According to Van Gennep, the duty of rites is that of protecting the individual in the passage phase. (passage rites)

仪式被视为克服日常困难的一种手段，这些困难通常由传统行为模式所引发。仪式的重复被视为缓解焦虑的工具，因为它能营造一种安全感，并建立与过去的联系。根据范·热内普的观点，仪式的职责在于保护个体度过过渡阶段。（过渡仪式）

## Social order guarantee 社会秩序的保障

Rites help to protect the social group from external dangers (the enemy or whatever threatens the integrity of the group) and internal dangers (conflicting disagreements between the members themselves) and to maintain balance. Radcliffe-Brown sustains that the functionality of rites is that “of maintaining and transmitting from one generation to another the emotional dispositions of which the very existence of the society depends, as it is established”. (“The Andaman islanders” 1922)

仪式有助于保护社会群体免受外部危险（敌人或任何威胁群体完整性的因素）和内部危险（成员之间的冲突与分歧）的侵害，并维持平衡。拉德克利夫-布朗认为，仪式的功能在于“维护并代代相传那些决定着社会存续的情感倾向，因为社会的存在正是建立在这些倾向之上的”。（《安达曼群岛居民》，1922年）

## The cognition role 认知作用

Rites tend to put into evidence the different aspects of life, not just that which seems contradictory or without a clear sense in the society or in nature, but also that which results problematical in the internal experience of the individuals. According to Turner, an author of whom I will take more into consideration further ahead, dramatization and representation form the element which “permits the ability to put light onto a conflict (which, moreover, is known collectively) and to clarify the nature of it, also by working with rational methods to be able to establish responsibility, sanctions and remedies. The importance of this cognitive aspect is testified by the ‘mdembu’ idea in which the rite unmasks, reveals and restores to the public that which is private, hidden”. (“The drum of affliction” 1968)

仪式往往旨在揭示生活的不同方面，不仅包括社会或自然中看似矛盾或缺乏明确意义的事物，还包括那些在个体内部体验中引发困惑的事物。据特纳（Turner）所言——我将在后文中进一步探讨这位作者的观点——戏剧化和再现构成了“能够揭示冲突（而且这种冲突是集体所知的）并阐明其本质，同时通过运用理性方法来确立责任、制裁和补救措施”的要素。“恩登布”（mdembu）这一理念印证了这一认知层面的重要性：仪式将私密、隐蔽之事揭露、显明并呈现给公众。（《苦难之鼓》，1968）

## The communicative role 沟通功能

Rites are seen as an activity characterised from the fact of “saying something” about the individuals involved in the action, “in particular by pointing out the status-role and making clear the social structure of the group. In rites, an information code of vital importance is expressed in the society that put it into practice”. (Leach 1968)

仪式被视为一种以“表达”参与该行动的个体为特征的活动，“特别是通过指明其地位与角色，并阐明群体的社会结构。在仪式中，实践该仪式的社会所表达的信息代码具有至关重要的意义”。（利奇 1968）

## Guarantee of continuity of the experience 体验连续性的保障

The role of a rite, according to Levi-Strauss, is to put into evidence that “whereas myth turns its back on continuity to outline the world by way of distinctions, contrasts and oppositions, rites follow an inverse movement… .like a complicated game and fundamentally irrational, contrary to mythical thoughts, they remain, however, indispensable because they introduce into any enterprise, however serious, an element of slowness and reflection, pauses and intermediate stops and in this way the ability to strengthen even war”. (“The bare man” 1971)

据列维-斯特劳斯所言，仪式的作用在于凸显这样一个事实：“神话背离连续性，通过区分、对比和对立来勾勒世界；而仪式则遵循一种相反的运动…… ……就像一场复杂的游戏，且本质上是非理性的；尽管与神话思维背道而驰，但它们却不可或缺，因为它们能为任何事业——无论多么严肃——注入一种缓慢与沉思、停顿与过渡的元素，从而甚至能增强战争的力量”。（《赤裸的人》1971）

## The unconscious significance 无意识的意义

Rites permit communication by way of unspoken symbols, where unconscious contents of individuals and groups find an expression because, being unconscious, escape verbalisation. “The symbols in a rite are symbols of an unconscious reality where this can only be grasped by way of the symbols themselves”. (A. Ge111975)

仪式通过无言的符号实现沟通，个体和群体的无意识内容得以在此表达，因为这些内容正因其无意识的性质而无法用语言表述。“仪式中的符号是无意识现实的象征，而这种现实只能通过符号本身来把握。”（A. Ge111975）

From a psychoanalytical point of view, moreover, rites have been associated to a neurotic behaviour. The obsessive repetition of certain stereotyped acts (continual hand washing or controlling of gas, ..) is considered as a ritualistic procedure. Freud writes: “one could venture to consider that obsessive neurosis is the pathological equivalent to religious formation, and to describe neurosis as an individual religiousness or religion as a universal obsessive neurosis”. (“Obsessive actions and religious practice” 1907)

此外，从精神分析的角度来看，仪式常被视为一种神经症行为。对某些刻板行为的强迫性重复（如不断洗手或检查煤气等）被视为一种仪式化程序。弗洛伊德写道：“人们或许可以大胆地认为，强迫性神经症是宗教形成的病理等价物，并将神经症描述为一种个体宗教性，或者将宗教描述为一种普遍的强迫性神经症。” （《强迫行为与宗教实践》1907）

Other exponents of analytic psychology have, instead, interpreted the rite as a means which can hold an individual in the transition phase that goes from one way of being to another. The rite maintains a psychic balance during the transformation phase.

而分析心理学的其他代表人物则将仪式解读为一种手段，能够使个体在从一种存在方式向另一种存在方式过渡的阶段中保持稳定。仪式在转变阶段维持着心理平衡。

# The Passage Rites 过渡仪式

I would now like to examine closely certain concepts of Van Gennep’s thoughts that are needed to reflect on the association between rite and theatre. He introduces the idea of “margin”, ’a concept which is needed to explain the idea of “liminality” introduced by Turner on the role of rite in the theatre. The “margin” concept refers to a border line more or less clear and accurate, a threshold, a neutral zone or no-one’s land, a physical element or whatever divides two spaces in which there is a passage.

现在，我想仔细探讨范·热内普思想中某些概念，这些概念对于反思仪式与戏剧之间的关联是必不可少的。他提出了“边缘”这一概念，该概念对于阐释特纳关于仪式在戏剧中作用所提出的“阈限”概念是必要的。“边缘”概念指的是一条或多或少清晰明确的分界线，一个门槛、一个中立区或无人区，一种物理要素，或是任何将两个存在过渡关系的空间分隔开来的事物。

“The margin phenomenon may be noticed in many other activities and could find itself in general biological activity, in physical energy applications, in cosmic rhythms”, (“The passage rites” 1981)

“边缘现象在许多其他活动中都能被察觉，它普遍存在于生物活动、物理能量的应用以及宇宙节律之中。”（《过渡仪式》，1981）

The social and biological activities are subject to wear and tear: this is why pauses are necessary to regenerate one’s strength. “It’s exactly this fundamental necessity that definitely corresponds to passage rites”. (“The passage rites” 1981) Passage rites connect society to nature, or more precisely, they introduce an aspect or a natural dimension to one’s social life.

社会活动和生物活动都会经历消耗：正因如此，停顿才成为恢复元气所必需的。“正是这种根本性的必要性，与过渡仪式有着确切的对应关系。”（《过渡仪式》，1981年）过渡仪式将社会与自然相联结，或者更准确地说，它们为个人的社会生活引入了一种自然维度。

In any type of society, explains Van Gennep, the individual finds himself faced with moments of change from one situation to another, which could be compared to the passage from one age to another or from one occupation to a new one. These passages are characterised by particular acts such as, in the case of a profession, to serve an apprenticeship or in the case of semi-civilised populations, fulfill particular religious ceremonies.

范·热内普解释道，在任何类型的社会中，个体都会面临从一种状况向另一种状况转变的时刻，这可以比作从一个年龄阶段过渡到另一个，或是从一种职业转向新的职业。这些过渡阶段具有特定的行为特征，例如在职业领域，需要完成学徒期；而在半文明族群中，则需履行特定的宗教仪式。

“Any change in the condition of the individual therefore implies actions and reactions which go between profane and sacred; these actions and reactions need to be regulated and controlled so that society in general is not subject to inconvenience or damage. It is the fact itself of living that makes the next passage necessary, that is, that which goes from one social situation to another in such a way that the individuals’ life carries out a sequence of stops and in which the final and first ones form a whole of the same order: birth, social puberty, marriage, parenthood, class progression, job specialisation, death. Each of these wholes correspond to ceremonies in which the finality is identical: to pass the individual from one determined situation to another situation, again determined”. (“The passage rites” 1981)

“因此，个体状况的任何变化都意味着在世俗与神圣之间往来的行动与反应；这些行动与反应需要受到规范和控制，以免给整个社会带来不便或损害。正是生存这一事实本身，使得下一次过渡成为必要，即从一种社会处境向另一种社会处境的过渡——个体的人生由此经历一系列停驻点，其中最初与最终的停驻点构成同一序列的整体：出生、社会青春期、婚姻、为人父母、阶级晋升、职业专业化、死亡。这些整体中的每一个都对应着某种仪式，其目的完全相同：将个体从一种既定的处境过渡到另一种同样既定的处境。”（《过渡仪式》1981）

He distinguishes three different typologies of passage rites:

他将过渡仪式分为三种不同的类型：

1. preliminary rites or rites of separation from previous surroundings - which make the detachment of the individual from an original situation easier. 预备仪式或脱离原有环境的仪式——这些仪式有助于个人更轻松地脱离原有处境。
2. liminary or marginal rites - which settle into a state of suspension or transition. 阈限仪式或边缘仪式——这些仪式使个人进入一种悬置或过渡的状态。
3. post-liminary rites or of aggregation - that support the individuals’ introduction into new territory, into a new group or into a new social category. 后阈限仪式或融入仪式——这些仪式支持个人融入新领域、新群体或新的社会类别。

# The rite and the theatre 仪式与戏剧

He sustains that the “representation” can be analysed by the role that is undertaken in daily life. Theatre could be seen as a phenomenon which he defines as ‘liminal’ (from the word ‘limen’ which signifies threshold, chosen by Van Gennep to indicate ‘transition between’) and which refers to the marginal or passing zone from defined social and cultural situations to new aggregations that change order. But what the author underlines is that these stages of passage are characterised as moments of change and transformation, filled with creative potential and of rebellion, not just on a large scale but also in micro-society as that of tribes. While he refers to the agricultural and tribal cultures as ‘liminal’, he uses a new term when he refers to the complex industrial society, and that is ‘liminoid’ (which is similar to liminal). Liminoid refers to the more technically complex society where free time and pastimes are separated from the organised life, that is time spent working. He points out a distinction between liminal and ‘liminoid’, which I will try to show by drawing up a parallel scheme of the two phenomena.

他认为，“表演”可以通过日常生活中所扮演的角色来分析。戏剧可以被视为一种现象，他将其定义为“阈限”（源自“limen”一词，意为门槛，范·热内普选用该词以表示“之间的过渡”），指从既定的社会和文化情境向改变秩序的新聚合体过渡的边缘或过渡地带。但作者强调的是，这些过渡阶段的特征在于它们是充满创造潜能与叛逆精神的变革与转型时刻，这种变革不仅发生在宏观层面，也存在于部落等微观社会中。虽然他将农业和部落文化称为“阈限”（liminal），但在论及复杂的工业社会时，他使用了一个新术语，即“阈限态”（liminoid）（与“阈限”相似）。““阈限态”指代技术上更为复杂的社会，其中闲暇时间与消遣活动与有组织的生活——即工作时间——相分离。他指出了“阈限态”与“阈限”之间的区别，我将尝试通过绘制这两个现象的并列图表来阐明这一点。

Turner therefore attributes to the theatre a decisive role in giving form to moments of passage, as moments of contact and encounters between actors and the public and as a privileged place to break the social schemes acquired.

因此，特纳认为戏剧在塑造过渡时刻方面发挥着决定性作用——这些时刻既是演员与观众之间接触和相遇的契机，也是打破既定社会模式的独特场所。

“I would like to consider the ritual most of all as a performance, a representation and not as a set of rules or pages … the term ‘performance’ derives from the old French word ‘parfournir’ which literally means ‘to provide in a complete and thorough way’. To perform, therefore, signifies producing something, completing something, or to carry out a drama, an order or a project. I think, though, that during the ‘execution’ something new can be produced. The performance transforms itself. Certainly, as I said previously, the rules can ‘frame them’, but the flow of action and interaction inside this frame can give rise to intuitions without precedent and may generate symbols and new meanings and incorporate them in following performances”. (“From rite to theatre” p.145)

“我最希望将仪式视为一种表演、一种呈现，而非一套规则或文字……‘表演’一词源自古法语‘parfournir’，字面意思是‘以完整而彻底的方式提供’。因此，表演意味着创造某种东西、完成某种事情，或是演绎一出戏剧、执行一项指令或实施一个项目。不过，我认为在‘执行’过程中可以产生新的东西。表演本身会发生转变。诚然，正如我之前所说，规则可以‘框定’它们，但这一框架内的行动与互动的流动，可以催生前所未有的直觉，并可能产生符号与新含义，进而将其融入后续的表演之中。”（《从仪式到戏剧》第145页）

Turner is not interested in the representation itself in daily life or daily life as a representation, as rejection that is created between person and role and collective multiplication of this rejection as a moment of liberation of the first from second, those moments of crisis that break generalization and the masks and investigating between instinct and reason.

特纳并不关注日常生活中的表征本身，也不关注日常生活作为一种表征；他关注的是人与其角色之间产生的排斥，以及这种排斥在集体层面的放大——这正是前者从后者中获得解放的瞬间；那些打破泛化与面具的危机时刻，以及在本能与理性之间进行的探索。

…“It’s the subjects, the actors as protagonists that, working inside the cultural structures, follow certain values and certain pre-arranged scripts; but they will never find themselves repeating only the character that way, that is, as nature wants and culture imposes: they will invent a language which will express in that moment a variation of the sketch and, at the same time, an opening to totality. The pre-arranged script is nature and liberty is culture”. (idem)

……“正是这些主体——作为主角的演员们——在文化结构中运作，遵循着某些价值观和某些预先安排好的剧本；但他们绝不会仅仅以那种方式重复角色，即顺应自然、服从文化强加的方式：他们将发明一种语言，这种语言在当下既表达了剧本的变奏，同时也开启了通向整体性的通道。预先安排的剧本即是自然，而自由即是文化”。（同上）

# Thoughts of V. Turner and vision of the Playback Theatre 特纳的思想与一人一故事剧场的愿景

We have seen how, for Turner, the theatrical performance assumes a decisive importance in the evolution of community, characterising itself as a privileged modality to be able to get to the point of transformations or redefine new orders. As I underlined previously, he affirms that performance could refer to the ‘margin’ phase, that is those moments, for the societies technologically more simple, which marks the passage from a certain social situation to a new one (passage rites- liminal phase).

我们已经看到，在特纳看来，戏剧表演在社群演变过程中具有决定性的重要性，它被视为一种独特的表现形式，能够直指变革的核心或重新定义新的秩序。正如我此前强调的，他认为表演可以指代“边缘”阶段，即在技术上较为简单的社会中，那些标志着从某种社会状况向新社会状况过渡的时刻（过渡仪式——阈限阶段）。

These rites had the role of introducing a natural dimension to the social life and permits those involved to live the change with more awareness and participation by way of a ceremony and a series of predefined acts. Passage rites responded to the need of security, to bonds with the past and at the same time offered cultural models as proposed by tradition.

这些仪式的作用在于为社会生活引入一种自然维度，并通过仪式和一系列预先定义的行动，使参与者能够以更清醒的意识和更积极的参与态度来经历这种变化。过渡仪式既满足了人们对安全感的需求，维系了与过去的纽带，同时也提供了传统所倡导的文化模型。

In more evolved and complex societies, lifetime is passed differently, that is, in a functional way parallel to the modern social organisation, where ceremonies and rites are not an integral part in people’s lives. The social practices and moments in which communities get together only take place in free time or as a pastime. It is in the ‘fiction’ of the theatre that the individual and the community can therefore try and find that which ritual ceremonies offered to the more simple man. Turner therefore sustains that the answer to this human need of change and to individuate new models should be looked for in the theatre.

在更发达、更复杂的社会中，人们度过一生方式有所不同，即以一种与现代社会组织并行的功能性方式，此时仪式和典礼已不再是人们生活不可或缺的组成部分。社群成员聚在一起的社会实践和活动，仅在闲暇时间或作为消遣时才发生。正是在戏剧的“虚构”之中，个体与社群才能尝试寻找那些仪式曾为原始人提供的东西。因此，特纳认为，对人类这种寻求变化和确立新模式的需求，其答案应从戏剧中寻找。

The theatrical performance in modern society has the same function as passage rites in more simple societies. According to the anthropologist, it’s up to the actor to ‘break up the script’.

在现代社会中，戏剧表演与原始社会中的过渡仪式具有相同的功能。据这位人类学家所言，“打破剧本”的责任在于演员。

From the moment that any interpretation can never be the same as the preceding one, the actor can always generate something new and assume a decisive role to indicate to the community new directions and changes. The theatre acquires a decisive role in giving form to these moments of passage and in the moment of contact or dispute between actors and public.

正因为任何一次演绎都绝不会与前一次完全相同，演员总能创造出新意，并发挥决定性作用，为社群指明新的方向与变革。在赋予这些过渡时刻以形式，以及在演员与观众接触或产生争论的瞬间，戏剧发挥着决定性作用。

How can you insert Playback Theatre compared with this theory?

结合这一理论，如何将一人一故事剧场置于其中？

I think that Playback constitutes an original form of synthesis between the original role of rite and the more modern form. Taking up again the scheme previously shown regarding the comparison between ‘liminal’ and ‘liminoide’, I see that in certain aspects Playback Theatre can be defined as a phenomenon of the ‘liminal’ type.

我认为，一人一故事剧场构成了仪式原始功能与现代形式之间的一种独特综合形式。重新审视前文关于“阈限”（liminal）与“阈限态”（liminoide）比较的框架，我发现一人一故事剧场在某些方面可被定义为某种“阈限”类型的现象。

The characteristics that mark Playback Theatre from other forms of theatre, and that collocate it nearer to ritual experiences of more simple societies (‘liminal’) in my opinion are:

在我看来，使一人一故事剧场区别于其他戏剧形式，并使其更接近简单社会中“阈限”仪式体验的特征包括：

1. Being a collective experience without having a mass effect (point 2) In Playback Theatre the subject that lives the experience is the community (continuative and temporary) that participates at the performance or performances. It has a character limited to the number of participants that is hardly more than a hundred people. It is an experience that is only significant for the community involved and only in the specific moment in which it is carried out. 作为一种集体体验，却不具有大众效应（第2点）在一人一故事剧场中，亲历这种体验的主体是参与演出（或系列演出）的社群（无论是持续存在的还是临时组建的）。其规模受参与者人数限制，通常不超过一百人。这种体验仅对参与其中的社区具有意义，且仅在演出进行的特定时刻才具有意义。
2. It reflects the history of the qroup and its collective experience with time (point 3)
Playback Theatre establishes an efficient means of exploration of the history of the individuals and of the group. It is also a powerful builder of community spirit. The single stories widen one’s boundaries and, by proposing new meanings, reach towards collectivity. In the case of community that already have a collective history, Playback Theatre becomes a means of dialogue with one’s self and reflection by means of the process of the ‘stories’. 它反映了群体的历史及其随时间积累的集体体验（第3点）。一人一故事剧场为探索个体与群体的历史提供了一种有效途径，同时也是凝聚社区精神的强大工具。单个故事能拓宽个人的认知边界，并通过提出新的意义，延伸至集体层面。对于已经拥有集体历史的社区而言，一人一故事剧场通过“故事”这一过程，成为一种与自我对话和反思的媒介。
3. Not being connected to social critics or revolutionary proclamations (point 5)
The purpose of Playback Theatre is that of honouring peoples’ stories, whatever they are. Judgement regarding the expressed subject doesn’t exist, nor does ideology in Playback Theatre. 不涉及社会批判或革命宣言（第5点）。一人一故事剧场的宗旨在于尊重人们的故事，无论这些故事是什么。在一人一故事剧场中，既不存在对所表达主题的评判，也不存在意识形态。

I think that these aspects, as well as being a form of non-written theatre therefore only transmissible orally, make Playback Theatre very similar to ancient forms of ritual performances that were at the base of social life in the community.

我认为，这些特点，加上其作为一种非文字剧场形式（因此只能通过口头传承）的特性，使得一人一故事剧场与古代仪式表演形式极为相似——而这些仪式表演曾是社群社会生活的基石。

Taking up Turner’s thoughts again which attribute to modern theatre, in particular to theatrical experimentation, a fundamental ritual for the role assumed in daily life to give form to changes and transformations, Playback can be depicted as a new manner particularly indicated to help realize the meeting between the actors and public, to link the experiences of one person to that of another, to involve the individual and community, to create a deep community spirit and to promote development and the change within itself.

再次援引特纳（Turner）的观点——他认为现代戏剧，尤其是戏剧实验，是日常生活中所扮演角色的根本性仪式，旨在为变化与转化赋予形式——一人一故事剧场可以被视为一种特别适合促进演员与观众之间互动的新方式，它能将一个人的经历与另一个人的经历联系起来，让个人和社区共同参与，营造深厚的社区精神，并促进社区内部的发展与变革。

Playback Theatre honours peoples’ stories and the possibility to stage daily life and is a unique and original method to carry out that which Turner sustains in this statement that concludes his text “From rite to theatre”. “And when we enter in any theatre in which our life grants access, we have already learnt how strange and stratified daily life is, how extraordinary that which is ordinary. So we don’t need the ‘infinite security’ of the ideologies any more, but we must appreciate the ‘unnecessary risk’ of theatrical action and interaction”. (Turner 1986, p. 214).

一人一故事剧场尊重人们的故事，重视将日常生活搬上舞台的可能性，是一种独特而独创的方法，旨在践行特纳在其文章《从仪式到戏剧》结尾处所阐述的观点。“当我们走进任何生活允许我们进入的剧场时，我们早已领悟到日常生活是多么奇特且层次丰富，平凡之事又是多么非凡。因此，我们不再需要意识形态所提供的‘无限安全感’，而是必须珍视戏剧行动与互动所带来的‘不必要的风险’。”（特纳 1986，第214页）。
