# Excerpt from A Playback Theatre Performance 摘自一场一人一故事剧场演出

The following is an edited and condensed transcript of a Playback Theatre performance that took place in June 1981, at the Mid-Hudson Arts and Science Center in Poughkeepsie, N.Y., Playback’s home base. The space-a large, bare, high-ceilinged room with large open windows-is filled to capacity, about 65 spectators. The conductor is Jonathan Fox, founder and director of Playback Theatre. When the audience enters and takes their seats, the company is hidden on a high balcony. The musician plays an out-of-tune fanfare on a slide-whistle, and all descend the stairs noisily. The actors sit on a semi-circle of wooden boxes upstage. The musician sits stage left and the Conductor stage right.

以下是1981年6月在纽约州波基普西市“中哈德逊艺术与科学中心”（一人一故事剧场的大本营）举行的一场一人一故事剧场演出的编辑精简版文字记录。演出场地——一个宽敞、空旷、层高挑且配有大型落地窗的大厅——座无虚席，约有65名观众。领航员是一人一故事剧场的创始人兼艺术总监乔纳森·福克斯。当观众入场就座后，剧团成员隐身于高处的包厢中。乐师用滑音哨吹奏了一段走调的号角曲，随后众人喧闹着走下楼梯。演员们坐在舞台后方的木箱半圆阵中。乐师坐在舞台左侧，领航员则坐在舞台右侧。

**CONDUCTOR:** (*Enthusiastically.*) Welcome to Playback Theatre, everybody! (*Applause.*) For those of you who are here for the first time, we tell stories about moments of our lives. For example, what happened to you today? Were you happy? Were you sad? What did you do today, did anything happen that was special? (*Pause.*) What was your day like?

**领航员：**（*热情洋溢地。*）欢迎大家来到一人一故事剧场！（*掌声。*）对于第一次来这里的观众，我们要讲述生活中那些瞬间的故事。比如，今天你经历了什么？你开心吗？难过吗？今天做了什么，有没有发生什么特别的事情？（*停顿。*）你今天过得怎么样？

**AUDIENCE MEMBER:** Good.

**观众：** 挺好的。

**CONDUCTOR:** Yes? What did you do that was good?

**领航员：** 是吗？你做了什么好事呢？

**AUDIENCE MEMBER:** I rode a bicycle to work.

**观众：** 我骑自行车去上班了。

**CONDUCTOR:** What’s your name?

**领航员：** 你叫什么名字？

**AUDIENCE MEMBER:** Mike.

**观众：** 迈克。

**CONDUCTOR:** Mike. (*To audience*.) Mike feels good because he rode a bicycle to work today. (*To Mike*.) Watch.

**领航员：** 迈克。（*对观众说*。）迈克今天心情很好，因为他是骑自行车去上班的。（*对迈克说*。）请看。

(*One by one, five or six actors move to center stage and make a sound-and-movement image expressive of Mike’s response, each building on the other’s actions. This short, animated action, accompanied by music, takes about one minute; it is called a fluid sculpture. The audience applauds.*)

(*五、六位演员依次走到舞台中央，通过声音和动作表现迈克的反应，每人都在前一位的动作基础上进行延伸。这段短小生动的动作配合音乐，持续约一分钟；它被称为“流动塑像”。观众鼓掌。*)

**CONDUCTOR:** Was it anything like that, Mike? (*Laughter from audience.*)

**领航员：** 迈克，你当时的感觉和这差不多吗？（*观众笑声。*）

**MIKE:** Yes.

**迈克：** 是的。

**CONDUCTOR:** You can say no, you know. (*Surveys audience.*) Who had a different kind of day today?

**领航员：** 你们可以拒绝哦，知道吗。（*环视观众。*）今天过得和往常不一样的人举手？

**AUDIENCE MEMBER:** I was so bored that I got depressed and went to sleep.

**观众：** 我无聊得要命，结果心情低落，最后睡着了。

**CONDUCTOR:** So bored that you got depressed and went to sleep... are you on vacation already? (*Laughter. Audience member smiles and nods.*) Watch.

**领航员：** 无聊到心情低落，最后睡着了……你已经开始度假了吗？（*笑声。观众微笑着点头。*） 请看。

(*Actors create another fluid sculpture. There is another exchange with an audience member, followed by a third fluid sculpture. After this, the company members introduce themselves. Following each name, the musician plays a short phrase on the slide whistle, different for each actor.*)

(*演员们再次摆出流动塑像。与一位观众进行了另一段互动，随后呈现了第三组流动塑像。之后，剧团成员们依次自我介绍。每念完一个名字，乐师就会用滑音哨吹奏一段乐句，每个演员的乐句都不一样。*)

**CONDUCTOR:** Now, I said before that we were going to be telling stories tonight. You know, nowadays people aren’t used to telling stories the way they were in the old days. Now we read, and we watch, but we don’t tell so much. So before we go on, I would like to invite you to tell a story to someone sitting next to you, and if you don’t know them, introduce yourself to them, because this is a theatre of neighbors rather than strangers. So share with anyone around you something that happened to you this week, and if you know the person, tell them something they don’t already know. Go ahead, try it out!

**领航员：** 刚才我说过，今晚我们要讲故事。你们知道吗，如今人们已经不习惯像从前那样讲故事了。现在我们读书、看剧，却很少自己讲故事。所以在继续之前，我想邀请大家给身边的人讲个故事；如果不认识对方，请先自我介绍一下，因为这里是邻里剧场，而非陌生人的聚会。请和身边的人分享本周发生在你身上的事；如果认识对方，就告诉他/她一些他/她还不知道的事情。来吧，试试看！

(*The audience tells each other stories. The volume increases as people get more involved in telling and hearing. After a few minutes, the Conductor interrupts.*)

(*观众们互相讲述故事。随着大家越来越投入地讲述和聆听，现场的喧哗声逐渐增大。几分钟后，领航员打断了大家。*)

**CONDUCTOR:** Now that wasn’t much time, but were you able to do it?

**领航员：** 虽然时间不长，但大家做到了吗？

**AUDIENCE MEMBER:** No, just getting warmed up.

**观众：** 没有，才刚热身呢。

**CONDUCTOR:** Just getting warmed up. Good! Now you know our secret! (*Points to chair beside him.*) Well, this chair we call the Teller’s chair, and that’s for someone who wants to share with all of us a moment-perhaps one of those moments you didn’t get to finish telling-and see it acted out on the spot. So who would like to be the first Teller tonight here at Playback Theatre?

**领航员：** 才刚热身呢。很好！现在你们知道我们的秘密了！（*指向身旁的椅子。*）这把椅子我们称之为讲述者之椅，它是为那些想要与大家分享某个瞬间的人准备的——也许正是你们刚才没来得及讲完的那个瞬间——并亲眼看到它当场被演绎出来。那么，今晚在一人一故事剧场，谁愿意成为第一位讲述者呢？

(*Several people raise their hands. The Conductor chooses a nine-year-old girl, who tells a story about a mean teacher. The actors enact the scene. The actor playing the teacher includes the audience in her menacing regard. At the conclusion of the scene, the Teller returns smiling to her seat. Another comes forward, this time a man who tells about riding his moped to work on a rainy day, dressed in yellow rain gear. He is mocked by Poughkeepsie’s eccentric street people because he looks like the Gorton’s Fishsticks man. The scene ends at a boring meeting presided over by the County Executive, which the actors produce non-literally: Three of them convey the mood by huddling together chanting “nothing, nothing,” and the actor playing the County Executive talks in an endless monotone, using official-sounding jargon. The audience participates by becoming the sound of the rain at the appropriate time. The Teller, watching, laughs a lot, especially at the climax, when the Teller’s actor takes his yellow costume and silences the County Executive by smothering her with it.*)

(*几个人举起了手。领航员选了一位九岁的小女孩，她讲了一个关于一位刻薄老师的故事。演员们演绎了这一场景。饰演老师的演员用威胁的目光扫视着全场观众。场景结束时，讲述者微笑着回到座位上。接着又有人上前，这次是一位男子，他讲述了雨天穿着黄色雨衣骑轻便摩托车去上班的经历。他因长得像戈顿鱼条广告里的那个男人，而遭到波基普西那些古怪街头人士的嘲笑。场景以一场由县行政长官主持的枯燥会议结束，演员们通过非写实的方式呈现这一场景：其中三人挤作一团，齐声念叨“没什么，没什么”，以此传达会议氛围；而饰演县行政长官的演员则用官腔十足的术语，以单调乏味的语调滔滔不绝地讲个没完。观众通过在恰当的时机模仿雨声来参与其中。旁观的讲述者笑个不停，尤其是在高潮时刻——当时饰演讲述者的演员拿起自己的黄色戏服，用它捂住县行政长官的嘴，让她闭上了嘴。*)

**CONDUCTOR:** Did that capture the spirit of your experience?

**领航员：** 这是否准确捕捉到了故事的精髓？

**TELLER:** Yes. Especially at the end. It really covered the whole thing.

**讲述者：** 是的。尤其是结尾部分。这真的把整个过程都涵盖了。

**CONDUCTOR:** Thank you very much.

**领航员：** 非常感谢。

(*Laughter and applause as Teller returns to audience and actors put away cloth and boxes and sit down. Next was an interlude where children who had been in a Playback class took the stage. They gave a mini-performance, conducted by their teacher, a Playback actor. The audience was delighted by their skill and spontaneity. When this was over, 75 minutes had passed since the show began.*)

(*随着讲述者回到观众席，演员们收起彩布和箱子坐下，现场响起笑声和掌声。接下来是一个间奏环节，参加过一人一故事课程的孩子们登上了舞台。在他们的老师——一位一人一故事演员的指导下，孩子们进行了一场小型表演。观众们对他们的技巧和自发性赞不绝口。当这一环节结束时，距离演出开始已过去75分钟。*)

**CONDUCTOR:** We’re only going to do one or two more stories tonight. Who has a story that they’d feel bad if they didn’t tell? (*Audience member raises hand.*) Kenny? (*He comes to Teller’s seat.*) O.K. Your name is Kenny, and where does your story take place?

**领航员：** 今晚我们只再讲一两个故事了。谁有什么故事，如果不讲出来会觉得遗憾呢？（*观众举手。*）肯尼？（*他走到讲述者的座位旁。*）好的。你叫肯尼，你的故事发生在哪里？

**KENNY:** Well, it’s all about the Atlanta situation. This is something that has been bothering me... well, since it happened, it’s been bothering me.

**肯尼：** 嗯，这完全是关于亚特兰大那件事的。这事一直困扰着我……呃，自从发生以来，就一直困扰着我。

**CONDUCTOR:** How do you read about it, or see it, or hear about it? How do you follow the Atlanta situation?

**领航员：** 你是怎么读到、看到或听到的？你是怎么关注亚特兰大那件事的？

**KENNY:** I know about it through the media.

**肯尼：** 我是通过媒体知道的。

**CONDUCTOR:** Like through T.V. or... ?

**领航员：**比如通过电视，还是……？

**KENNY:** All the media.

**肯尼：**所有媒体。

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K. Pick someone to be you.

**领航员：**好的。选个人来扮演你吧。

**KENNY:** No. That’s not where I want the story to be.

**肯尼：**不。我不想让故事这样发展。

**CONDUCTOR:** I Know. That’s O.K., but I want someone to be you.

**领航员：** 我知道。没关系，但我还是希望有人来扮演你。

**KENNY:** Oh, O.K. Pete.

**肯尼：** 哦，好吧。皮特。

(*As Actors are chosen, they stand up, maintaining their presence and inwardly preparing for their roles.*)

(*演员选定后，他们站起身来，保持角色状态，并在内心为角色做准备。*)

**CONDUCTOR:** Because you’re seeing it and feeling it and experiencing it, even though you’re not there. You with me?

**领航员：** 因为你正在看到它、感受到它并亲身体验它，尽管你并不在现场。明白我的意思吗？

**KENNY:** Right.

**肯尼：** 没错。

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K. That’s why we pick someone to be you. That’s just to remind us that it’s through you that this is coming to us. What’s happening in Atlanta? Let’s pick a moment.

**领航员：** 好。这就是为什么我们要选一个人来代表你。这只是为了提醒我们，这一切是通过你传递给我们的。亚特兰大那边发生了什么？我们选一个瞬间吧。

**KENNY:** Oh, you mean you want me to pick... well, you’re kind of rearranging...I was thinking... What are you getting at, because I was going on a different way, I guess.

**肯尼：** 哦，你是说想让我选……嗯，你好像在调整方向……我原本在想……你到底想表达什么，因为我刚才的思路好像不太一样。

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K. Tell me how you were going, and I’ll see if I...

**领航员：** 好的。先告诉我你刚才的思路，我再看看我能不能……

**KENNY:** O.K. And then you can bring it back. I was going to talk about the situation, and how it’s been going, and then the other night there was a program, Nightline did a special, they thought they caught the man. But I didn’t think it’s an individual, I have a whole different idea about who’s doing it, but that’s... that might be irrelevant. But the last time I came to Playback, I came from Albany, and just as I got off the bus there was a march down Main Street. And there were about 20 black people, I’d say, and one or two white people. And I found out that the march was for the Atlanta children. So I decided that this is something I care about, this is something I was going to participate in.

**肯尼：** 好的。然后你可以把话题拉回来。我本来想谈谈当前的局势，以及事情的发展情况，还有前几天晚上有一档节目，《夜线》做了一期特别报道，他们以为抓到了那个男人。但我并不认为这是某个个人的所作所为，我对幕后黑手有完全不同的看法，不过……这可能无关紧要。不过上次我来一人一故事时，是从奥尔巴尼过来的，刚下公交车就看到主街上有一场游行。大概有20名黑人，我估计，还有一两个白人。后来我才知道，这场游行是为了亚特兰大的孩子们。所以我决定，这是我关心的事，我一定要参与其中。

**CONDUCTOR:** I want to stop you.

**领航员：**我想打断一下。

**KENNY:** O.K.

**肯尼：**好的。

**CONDUCTOR:** I’d like to do either that march or what you think is happening in Atlanta. Which do you feel is closer to you?

**领航员：**我想问的是，是那场游行，还是你认为亚特兰大正在发生的事情？你觉得哪一个与你更相关？

**KENNY:** Well, I think the march.

**肯尼：**嗯，我觉得是那场游行。

**CONDUCTOR:** And did you join it?

**KENNY:** Yes, I joined the march.

**领航员：**那你参加了游行吗？

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K. Let’s pick, let’s see, you said about 20 black people and one or two white people.

**领航员：**好的。我们来选一下，你刚才说大概有20名黑人和一两个白人。

**KENNY:** Yes, well, about that ratio.

**肯尼：**是的，大概就是那个比例。

**CONDUCTOR:** Pick someone to be the white person.

**领航员：**选一个人来扮演那个白人吧。

**KENNY:** Judy.

**肯尼：**朱迪。

**CONDUCTOR:** What’s a word for that white person who’s marching?

**领航员：**那个正在游行的白人可以用什么词来形容？

**KENNY:** Concerned.

**肯尼：**关切。

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K. And you joined them, right?

**领航员：**好的。你是加入了他们，对吧？

**KENNY:** I joined them, out of concern.

**肯尼：**我是出于关切才加入他们的。

**CONDUCTOR:** You’re getting off a bus in Poughkeepsie, and you see the marchers, and what happens?

**领航员：**你在波基普西下车时，看到了游行队伍，然后发生了什么？

**KENNY:** I join the march, and I start talking to a young man about my age, who started explaining to me what was going on, and then we started talking more about the Atlanta situation, and we both pretty much had the same view. I think it’s a conspiracy.

**肯尼：** 我加入了游行队伍，并开始和一个和我年纪相仿的年轻人交谈，他向我解释了当时的情况，随后我们进一步讨论了亚特兰大事件，我们的观点基本一致。我认为这是一场阴谋。

![](images28c0f544-f88a-42ef-af02-8ce40cbd9f66-10_794_1247_175_153.jpg)

Playback performers create a birthday party where there is a crisis about balloons.

表演者们策划了一场生日派对，派对上发生了关于气球的危机。

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K.

**领航员：** 好的。

**KENNY:** This is my opinion.

**肯尼：** 这是我的看法。

**CONDUCTOR:** O.K. And what happens at the end of the march?

**领航员：** 好的。那么游行结束时会发生什么？

**KENNY:** As the march progresses, what I see is people’s lack of understanding about what is happening in Atlanta. 50,000 kids are abducted each year, and in this situation we have 28 black youngsters.

**肯尼：** 随着游行的进行，我看到的是人们对亚特兰大正在发生的事情缺乏了解。每年有5万名儿童被绑架，而这次事件中涉及的却是28名黑人青少年。

**CONDUCTOR:** What do you think is happening?

**领航员：** 你觉得发生了什么？

**KENNY:** What I think is happening is that people... the white people aren’t concerned because these kids are not white, and the black people aren’t concerned because it’s not their turf. As we marched, we got views from people in the street, black as well as white, who wouldn’t join us for some reason.

**肯尼：** 我认为发生的情况是……白人并不关心，因为这些孩子不是白人；黑人也不关心，因为这不关他们的事。游行途中，我们看到街上的行人——无论黑人还是白人——出于某种原因都不愿加入我们。

**CONDUCTOR:** Let’s have a few people to be the non-joiners. (*Kenny picks three actors.*) And these people who haven’t been chosen can be the marchers. O.K.

**领航员：** 让我们选几个人来扮演那些不参与游行的人。（*肯尼选了三位演员。*）而那些没被选中的人可以扮演游行队伍。好的。

**KENNY:** And then it gathers down at the baseball field, where there is a rally of some sort. The mayor came and spoke but very very briefly. But also, right across the road was a big fair, and within me I find it hard to justify a fair, while something of this intensity is going on.

**肯尼：** 随后队伍聚集在棒球场，那里正在举行某种集会。市长来了并发表了讲话，但非常非常简短。不过，马路对面正举办着一场大型集市，我内心觉得，在如此紧张的局势下举办集市实在难以自圆其说。

**CONDUCTOR:** Good, so we’re going to have marchers. And we’re going to have you getting off the bus and joining the march; and there are going to be people on the street; and it’s going to end up at this rally.

**领航员：** 好，所以我们会安排游行队伍。你们将从公交车上下来加入游行；街上会有行人；最后会汇入这场集会。

**KENNY:** Right.

**肯尼：** 好的。

**CONDUCTOR:**... and there will be a fair going on. Let’s have this part of the audience (*Indicating.*) be the fair. You can feel it for yourselves when time comes. O.K.? Watch.

**领航员：**……届时集市仍在进行。让我们让这部分观众（*指着*）扮演集市。到时候你们自己就能体会到那种氛围。好吗？看好了。

(*The lights dim. The musician plays rhythmic percussion music, setting a mood of urgency. The actors go to the prop tree and choose pieces of cloth to wear; e.g. Judy, as the white marcher, accentuates her role by wearing a piece of white cloth. One of the onlookers wears a piece of cloth around his eyes, signifying blindness. As they are ready, the actors take positions on stage and remain still. When all are ready, the music ceases. The lights go up.*)

(*灯光变暗。乐师演奏节奏感强的打击乐，营造出一种紧迫感。演员们走到装置树旁，挑选彩布披在身上；例如，饰演白人游行者的朱迪，通过披上一块彩布来突出角色特征。一名围观者用一块彩布蒙住双眼，象征失明。准备就绪后，演员们在舞台上各就各位并保持静止。当所有人准备就绪时，音乐停止。灯光亮起。*)

**TELLER’S ACTOR:** (*Gets off bus, looking confused and amazed.*) Wow! What’s going on down there?

**讲述者演员：** （*走下巴士，一脸困惑和惊讶。*）哇！下面到底发生了什么？

(*Musician and other actors, using voices, create ominous sound effects. Marchers circle slowly around stage.*)

（*乐师和其他演员通过声音营造出不祥的音效。游行队伍在舞台上缓慢绕行。*）

**MARCHER 1:** We’re marching for the children. You want to join?

**游行者1：** 我们是为了孩子们而游行。你想加入吗？

**MARCHER 2:** We’re marching for the Atlanta children. It’s got to stop.

**游行者2：** 我们是为了亚特兰大的孩子们而游行。这种事必须停止。

**WHITE MARCHER:** We’ve got to show them that we care.
