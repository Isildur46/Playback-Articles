# CITIZEN ACTOR 公民演员

*Jonathan Fox (2020)*

*乔纳森·福克斯（2020）*

"CITIZEN ACTOR" was a term I used from the start of our work in the 70s, but I never elaborated on it until I held a workshop on the topic in Germany early in 2020. At the very end of the workshop, a participant politely asked me to once and for all define the term. This was my response.

“公民演员”是我从70年代开始工作时就使用的术语，但直到2020年初在德国举办关于该主题的工作坊之前，我从未对此进行过详细阐述。在工作坊的最后，一位参与者礼貌地请我一劳永逸地定义这个术语。以下便是我的回答。

Citizen actor is an evocative term, perhaps uniquely suited to the English language. It denotes "actor" in the standard meaning of a theatrical role player, and at the same time a suggestion of "activist." There is a long-standing debate in our theatre community around this question of activism, but whatever your view, Playback Theatre practice is committed to general principles of human rights, which includes the possibility of everyone having the chance to articulate their story in a public forum. Because of the ongoing suppression of certain groups in our societies, not to speak of the widespread skepticism about the idea of inalienable rights, any Playback Theatre actor will also be an activist if she is to live up to this credo of all humans deserving a voice, including women, children, and minorities.

“公民演员”是一个富有感染力的术语，或许特别适合英语表达。它既指代戏剧中扮演角色的“演员”这一标准含义，同时也暗含“行动者”的意味。在我们的戏剧社群中，关于“行动主义”这一议题一直存在长期争论，但无论你的观点如何，一人一故事剧场的实践都致力于捍卫人权的基本原则，其中包括让每个人都有机会在公共论坛上讲述自己的故事。鉴于社会中某些群体长期遭受压迫，更不用说人们对“不可剥夺的权利”这一理念普遍持怀疑态度，任何一人一故事剧场演员若要践行“所有人类——包括妇女、儿童和少数群体——都应拥有发声权”这一信条，就必然也会成为一名行动主义者。

"Citizen" implies is a sense of community responsibility, in the sense of the image of Benjamin Franklin founding the first lending library. This is congruent with the community focus of applied theatre and stands opposed to an art for art's sake philosophy, where the artist has principle allegiance to her own creation. The citizen actor will espouse an ethics that strives for a theatre event benefiting everyone involved.

“公民”一词蕴含着社群责任感，正如本杰明·富兰克林创立第一家借阅图书馆的形象所体现的那样。这与应用戏剧的社群导向相契合，并反对“为艺术而艺术”的哲学——在该哲学中，艺术家首要效忠于自己的创作。作为公民的演员将秉持一种伦理，致力于让戏剧活动惠及所有参与者。

Citizen also connotes a "common man." Even though the Playback actor has developed a high degree of skill, she does not hold herself apart, but melds into the social fabric when not on stage. This integration—the Playback actor as common citizen—requires humility, an absence of ego in social standing that mirrors the egolessness of our stage work, with its emphasis on collective creation. One can assume that the time spent by the Playback actor as an ordinary member of the community will help her grasp the stories of ordinary people as they are narrated on the Playback Theatre stage.

“公民”一词还蕴含着“普通人”的含义。尽管一人一故事演员已具备高超的表演技巧，但她并不自视高人一等，而在舞台之外，她会融入社会肌理之中。这种融合——一人一故事演员作为普通公民的存在——需要谦逊，需要摒弃社会地位带来的自我意识，这恰恰映照了我们舞台创作中强调集体创作的无我精神。可以推测，一人一故事剧场演员作为社群普通一员所度过的时间，将有助于她在一人一故事剧场的舞台上如实呈现普通人的故事。

The meaning of contemporary experience is often not visible without a sense of context that includes historical and political knowledge. Nor are the emotions of tellers and audiences manageable in a constructive manner without psychological knowledge. The citizen actor should educate herself in both these kinds of awareness.

若缺乏包含历史与政治知识的语境意识，当代经验的意义往往难以显现。同样，若缺乏心理学知识，讲述者与观众的情绪也难以得到建设性的引导。公民演员应当在上述两种意识方面不断自我修养。

Because those holding political power tend to write history to their own advantage, we are all indoctrinated during our formative years, especially perhaps if we benefit from social and economic privilege. It is also incumbent upon the citizen actor to interrogate herself regarding social stereotypes and prejudice that might contaminate understanding and enacting others' stories.

由于掌握政治权力的人往往倾向于为自身利益而书写历史，我们在成长过程中都受到某种程度的灌输，特别是当我们享有社会和经济特权时。公民演员还有责任审视自身，反思那些可能影响对他人故事的理解与演绎的社会刻板印象和偏见。

In summary, the citizen actor fulfills an artistic calling and more. We can encapsulate these meanings into a brief definition: A citizen actor is a creative artist dedicated to the betterment of self and society.

总而言之，公民演员不仅履行着艺术使命，更肩负着超越艺术的职责。我们可以将这些内涵浓缩为一个简短的定义：公民演员是致力于自我与社会共同进步的创造性艺术家。
