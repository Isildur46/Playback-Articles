# Playing the Other: the ethical limitations of playback performing 扮演他者：一人一故事表演的伦理局限

By Nick Rowe (2005)

> I have asked myself what I would do if, as a white, heterosexual, English, middle class man, I was cast as the teller’s actor when the teller was, say, a black New York rapper, or a woman who is openly expressive around sexuality. Would it be possible for me to do justice to their stories? I ask this because I think I would feel uncomfortable and inadequate representing their experience. Of course it is immediately obvious that my choice of tellers exposes my cultural limitations, assumptions and even ‘hang-ups’. Why did I choose those tellers in particular? I am sure that my choices tell you far more about me that they do about them. Nevertheless my dilemma does raise some issues that are critical for me as a playback actor.
>
> 我曾自问：如果作为一名白人、异性恋、英国籍、中产阶级的男性，我被选中扮讲述者的演员，而讲述者恰好是一位黑人纽约说唱歌手，或者是一位在性别方面坦率表达的女性，我会怎么做？我能否真正还原他们的故事？我提出这个问题，是因为我觉得若要呈现他们的经历，我会感到不自在且力不从心。当然，很明显，我选择的讲述者暴露了我的文化局限、先入之见，甚至“个人课题”。为什么我偏偏选择了这些讲述者？我确信，我的选择所反映出的更多是关于我自身的信息，而非关于他们。尽管如此，我的这一困境确实引发了一些对我作为一人一故事演员至关重要的问题。
> 
> If cast in these roles how should I react? I could, of course, refuse saying that I am unable to play such a role convincingly - that would I think be a cop-out and what is more important it would be a serious misunderstanding of playback performing….
>
> 如果被选中扮演这些角色，我该如何应对？当然，我可以以“无法令人信服地演绎此类角色”为由拒绝——但我认为这不过是推卸责任，更重要的是，这将是对一人一故事表演的严重误解……

In this article I will attempt to address the dilemma I have set myself above. I imagine that my questions are not unfamiliar to you: they go to the heart of the playback and bring into question the ethical responsibilities of the performer. The questions are raised: what are the limitations of the playback actor? Can we play a teller no matter how culturally unfamiliar to us? If we accept that our performances are always influenced by our culture and ideology then is it ethically acceptable for us to represent another? If it is ethically possible, then what might be some of the principles that would guide our performances?

在本文中，我将尝试探讨上述自我设定的困境。我想这些问题对你们来说并不陌生：它们直指一人一故事表演的核心，并引发了对表演者伦理责任的质疑。随之而来的问题是：一人一故事表演者的界限在哪里？无论讲述者的文化背景对我们多么陌生，我们能否演绎这个角色？如果我们承认自己的表演总是受到自身文化和意识形态的影响，那么我们代表他人进行表演在伦理上是否可接受？如果从伦理上讲是可行的，那么有哪些原则可以指导我们的表演呢？

These issues and questions are, of course, not new to the playback community. The March 1996 issue of Interplay (the newsletter of the International Playback Theatre Network) was a key one in marking a significant change in the development of playback with respect to issues of cultural diversity. With the subtitle ‘What special education will white companies and actors need?’ the movement seems to formally recognise for the first time the cultural limitations of the performer. More specifically Johnson asks,

当然，这些问题对一人一故事剧场社群来说并不新鲜。1996年3月刊的《Interplay》（国际一人一故事剧场网络的通讯期刊）是一期关键刊物，标志着一人一故事剧场在文化多样性问题上的发展发生了重大转变。该期杂志以“白人剧团和演员需要什么样的特殊培训？”为副标题，这一运动似乎首次正式承认了表演者的文化局限性。更具体地说，约翰逊问道：

> Where will white conductors get training in decoding the African-American schema and story so that actors can playback these stories with integrity? (1996 p.4)
>
> 白人领航员将从何处接受培训，以解读非裔美国人的思维模式和故事，从而使演员能够真实地做一人一故事？（1996年，第4页）

It appears that, at least in this publication, the playback community was, for the first time, confronting the cultural limitations of the playback theatre. The notion of the ‘authentic’ actor able to respond to any story, no matter how culturally ‘strange’ was being brought into question. This is an important moment in the development of playback theatre. For the first time they were asking: what are the limitations of playback acting? Can we assume that with good will and ‘authenticity’ an actor can take on any role no matter how culturally unfamiliar?

看来，至少在这份出版物中，一人一故事剧场社群首次直面了一人一故事剧的文化局限性。那种认为“真实的”演员能够回应任何故事——无论其文化背景多么“陌生”——的观念正受到质疑。这是一人一故事剧场发展历程中的重要时刻。他们首次提出这样的疑问：一人一故事表演的局限性究竟在哪里？我们能否假设，只要怀有善意并具备“真实性”，演员就能胜任任何角色，无论其文化背景多么陌生？

In order to get this question into focus it will be helpful to look at opposite sides of the debate. This will enable a clear view of the arguments for and against what we might call the possibility of the ‘universal performer’. Although this may prove a useful strategy it will be important to recognise that few of us would take up a trenchant position on either side. In fact most playback practitioners, I suspect, would respond by saying “It depends” - on the context of the performance, the nature of the audience and their relationship with the performers, the experience and cultural sensitivity of the performers and so on. Nevertheless the debate might be said to lie between the ‘organic approach’ to acting on one side and, what I will call, the ‘radical difference approach’ one on the other. In other words, between those who consider that the performer can discover organic truths through her authentic encounter onstage and those who would argue that whatever is produced by the actor is always inflected by culture and ideology.

为了厘清这一问题，审视辩论的双方观点将大有裨益。这将有助于我们清晰地看到关于所谓“普世表演者”可能性的正反论点。尽管这可能是一种有用的策略，但重要的是要认识到，我们当中很少有人会对任何一方采取尖锐的立场。事实上，我猜想大多数一人一故事实践者会回答说“这要看情况”——取决于表演的语境、观众的性质及其与表演者的关系、表演者的经验和文化敏感度等等。尽管如此，这场辩论可以说是在表演的“有机方法”与我称之为“根本差异方法”之间展开的。换言之，一方认为表演者可以通过在舞台上的真实遭遇发现有机真理，另一方则主张演员所呈现的一切总是受到文化和意识形态的影响。

## The ‘organic approach’ “有机方法”

There has been a thread running through dramatic theory and practice, certainly since Stanislavski, which Richard Drain (1995), in his anthology of twentieth-century theatre theory, terms the ‘Inner Dimension’. The demand for the revelation of the actor’s self on stage can be seen in the work of Artaud, Grotowski, Copeau, Brook and Julian Beck for example. In different ways these practitioners and theorists have sought to establish theatre practice which is wholly or largely based on the self-exploration of the performers. They have often contrasted their work with what they perceive to be a traditional or conventional theatre practice characterised by artifice, the subjection of the actor to the playwright or director, and the disappearance of the actor’s self behind the role. Jerzy Grotowski, one of the most rigorous exponents of ‘self revelation’ in acting writes:

自斯坦尼斯拉夫斯基以来，戏剧理论与实践中始终贯穿着一条主线，理查德·德雷恩（1995）在其编纂的《二十世纪戏剧理论选集》中将其称为“内在维度”。例如，在阿尔托、格罗托夫斯基、科波、布鲁克和朱利安·贝克的作品中，都可以看到对演员在舞台上展现自我的要求。这些实践者和理论家以不同的方式，试图建立一种完全或主要基于表演者自我探索的戏剧实践。他们常常将自己的工作与他们所认为的传统或常规戏剧实践进行对比——后者以做作、演员服从剧作家或导演，以及演员的自我在角色背后消失为特征。耶日·格罗托夫斯基作为表演中“自我揭示”理念最严谨的倡导者之一，曾写道：

> When I say that the action must engage the whole personality of the actor […] it is a question of the very essence of the actor’s calling, of a reaction on his part allowing him to reveal one after the other the different layers of his personality, from the biological-instinctive source via the channel of consciousness and thought, to that summit which is so difficult to define and in which all becomes unity. (1995 p.279-280)
> 
> 当我说表演必须调动演员的整个人格时……这关乎演员职业的本质，关乎他自身的一种反应，这种反应使他能够逐层揭示自己人格的不同层面，从生物本能的源头出发，经由意识和思想的渠道，直至那个难以定义的巅峰——在那里，一切都融为一体。（1995年，第279-280页）

Grotowski seems to be saying that there is, at the ‘summit’, a unified foundational self - a ‘soul’, perhaps - that becomes a ‘gift’ and ‘a provocation’ to the spectator. As Judith Butler might say, it is an ‘epistemological paradigm that presumes the priority of the doer to the deed’ (1990 p.148). It is the call for the ‘pure’ presence of the performer, which will enable transformative and authentic theatre (Auslander 1995).

格罗托夫斯基似乎在说，在那个“巅峰”之上，存在着一个统一的根本自我——或许可以称之为“灵魂”——它对观众而言既是一种“馈赠”，也是一种“挑衅”。正如朱迪斯·巴特勒所言，这是一种“假定行为者优先于行为的认识论范式”（1990年，第148页）。这是对表演者“纯粹”在场的呼唤，而这种在场将催生具有转化与真实性的戏剧（奥斯兰德，1995年）。

This approach to acting is often apparent in playback discourse. For example Jo Salas writes:

这种表演方式在一人一故事的讨论中常常显而易见。例如，乔·萨拉斯写道：

> Playback acting requires a particular kind of acting which we can call authentic acting, as opposed to the more stylised acting familiar from television, film and most other kinds of theatre, however naturalistic. In playback acting, the actor does not use a code to depict emotion but draws her portrayal directly from her sense of the teller and the story. (Salas 1999 p.25)
>
> 一人一故事表演需要一种特殊的表演方式，我们可以称之为“真实表演”，这与电视、电影以及大多数其他戏剧中常见的、更为风格化的表演方式形成对比——无论后者多么自然主义。在自发的即兴重现表演中，演员并不借助某种表现符号来描绘情感，而是直接从她对讲述者及故事的感知中汲取表演素材。（萨拉斯 1999，第25页）

And the IPTN describe the process in the following way:

而 IPTN 则以如下方式描述这一过程：

> Authenticity in the spontaneous moment underlies Playback theatre practice. (IPTN 1999)
> 
> 自发的即兴时刻中的真实性是一人一故事剧场实践的基础。（IPTN 1999）

These descriptions of playback acting imply that the ‘playing of the other’ is not necessarily problematic. If the actor is ‘authentic’ and ‘draws her portrayal from her sense of the teller and the story’ then it is likely that the portrayal will be truthful to the radical difference of the teller. Phillip Auslander writes that the problem with these conceptions is that, without considerable nuancing, they

这些关于一人一故事表演的描述暗示，“扮演他者”并不一定存在问题。如果演员是“真实的”，并且“从对讲述者及故事的感知中汲取表演灵感”，那么这种表演很可能真实地反映出讲述者与演员之间的根本差异。菲利普·奥斯兰德指出，这些观念的问题在于，如果不加以充分的细化，它们

> …designate the actor’s self as the logos of performance …and assume that the actor’s self precedes and grounds her performance and that it is the presence of the self in performance that provides the audience with access to human truths. (Auslander 1995 p.60)
> 
> …将演员的自我视为表演的“逻各斯”……并假定演员的自我先于并奠定了其表演的基础，且正是表演中自我的存在，才使观众得以接触人类的真理。（奥斯兰德 1995 年，第 60 页）

The question is: can we trust the actor’s self to that degree, especially when it comes to playing another who is significantly different? It supposes a true, universal self beyond culture and language: a position that is difficult to sustain given the impact of the poststructuralist rejection of univocal truth and stable meaning.

问题在于：我们能否在如此程度上信任演员的自我，尤其是当她饰演一个与自己截然不同的人时？这假设存在一个超越文化和语言的真实、普世的自我：鉴于后结构主义对单一真理和稳定意义的否定所产生的影响，这种立场很难站得住脚。

## The ‘playback apologist’ speaks “一人一故事辩护者”发声

This concept of authenticity is one for which I have a great deal of sympathy. I am aware of many times in which I have felt that I have revealed something of myself in portraying the teller’s story. Or at least, I have made use, for example, of my own experiences of depression, neurotic anxiety, loss or the joys of parenthood. I find myself in an uncomfortable position, since questioning these ideas of authenticity and self revelation expose me to the risk of ‘losing my footing’ as a playback performer. I confess to a fear that such an examination may lead me to destroy the beliefs that sustain me as an actor and make playback theatre such an important part of my personal and professional life.

我对这种真实性概念深表认同。我意识到，在演绎讲述者的故事时，我曾多次感到自己揭示了某种自我。或者至少，我曾利用过自己的经历——例如抑郁、神经质焦虑、失去亲人的痛苦，或是为人父母的喜悦。我发现自己处于一种尴尬的境地，因为质疑这些关于真实性与自我揭示的观念，会让我面临作为一人一故事表演者“失去立足点”的风险。我坦承自己担心，这种审视可能会让我摧毁那些支撑我作为演员的信念并使一人一故事剧场成为我个人和职业生活中如此重要的一部分。

## The ‘radical difference approach’ “根本差异方法”

This ‘approach’ comes from two main directions in order to critique the notion of the authentic actor who can depend on the ‘truth’ of the actors’ experience. The first direction stresses the radical, idiosyncratic and ineffable difference of the ‘other’. The second emphasises the inevitable cultural and ideological influences on all acting; this may be especially the case for improvised acting where, under pressure to do ‘something’, the dangers of slipping into cliché and stereotype are so present. I will look at each in turn.

这种“方法”源于两个主要方向，旨在批判那种能够依赖演员体验“真实性”的“真实演员”观念。第一种视角强调“他者”所具有的根本性、独特且难以言喻的差异。第二种视角则强调所有表演都不可避免地受到文化和意识形态的影响；这一点在即兴表演中尤为明显——在必须“做点什么”的压力下，滑向陈词滥调和刻板印象的风险始终如影随形。我将依次探讨这两者。

The first critique of the ‘organic’ approach focuses on the risks of eradicating the other through representation. The feminist Hélène Cixous warns of the delights and dangers of identification. Elin Diamond explains:

对“有机”方法的第一项批判聚焦于通过表征消解“他者”的风险。女权主义者埃莱娜·西苏（Hélène Cixous）警示了身份认同所带来的愉悦与危险。埃琳·戴蒙德（Elin Diamond）解释道：

> Cixous is describing the mimetic pleasures of identification - becoming or inhabiting the other on the stage or in spectatorial fantasy; I stand in for her, act in his place. Such acts are distinctly imperialistic and narcissistic: I lose nothing - there is no loss of self - rather I appropriate you, amplifying my ‘I’ into an authoritative ‘we’. (1999 p.390)
> 
> 西苏所描述的是身份认同带来的模仿式愉悦——在舞台上或观众的幻想中成为他者或栖居于他者之中；我代替她，代他出演。此类行为具有鲜明的帝国主义和自恋色彩：我毫无损失——没有自我丧失——相反，我将你据为己有，将我的“我”放大为权威的“我们”。（1999年，第390页）

Cixous’s concern is that the consequences of identification are the denial of difference and distance. I force myself into the teller, engulfing the subtleties of his experience, erasing his identity and replacing it with my own. I possess him. My empathy precludes his otherness. Because the processes of identification are so intense in playback this danger is always present. The presence of the teller and the need for the actor to improvise may potentially collapse the psychic distance between performer and character and between teller and actor. Far from offering a viewpoint on the idiosyncrasy of the other, empathy may occlude it. Differences of gender, ethnicity and sexuality may be erased in this process of identification.

西苏的担忧在于，身份认同的后果是否定差异与距离。我强行闯入讲述者之中，吞噬他经历中的细微之处，抹去他的身份并用我自己的身份取而代之。我占有他。我的共情排除了他的“他者性”。由于在一人一故事中身份认同的过程如此强烈，这种危险始终存在。讲述者的在场以及演员即兴发挥的必要性，可能会瓦解表演者与角色之间、讲述者与演员之间的心理距离。共情非但未能展现他者的独特性，反而可能将其遮蔽。性别、种族和性取向的差异都可能在这个身份认同过程中被抹去。

Cixous is not alone in her concerns; the point is compellingly made by bell hooks in a passage which could be read as a forceful and biting critique of playback.

持有这种担忧的并非只有西苏斯；贝尔·胡克斯在一段文字中对此进行了极具说服力的阐述，这段文字可被解读为对一人一故事表演的一种有力而尖锐的批判。

> No need to hear your voice. Only tell me about your pain. I want to know your story. And then I will tell it back to you in a new way. Tell it back to you in such a way that it has become mine, my own. Rewriting you, I write myself anew. I am still author, authority. I am still the coloniser, the speaking subject, and you are now in the centre of my talk.(1990 p.152)
> 
> 没必要听你的声音。只要告诉我你的痛苦。我想了解你的故事。然后，我会用一种崭新的方式将它复述给你听。以一种使它成为我自己的、属于我自己的方式复述给你听。在重写你的同时，我也重新书写了自己。我依然是作者，是权威。我依然是殖民者，是发声的主体，而你现在却成了我话语的中心。（1990年，第152页）

A playback performance to delegates at a Conference in Eastbourne in May 2004 exemplifies these concerns. During the enactment of a story concerning the problems encountered by a man travelling by train with a disabled friend, an actor represented the disabled man as passive and suggested through his speech and movement that he may also have a learning disability. In his feedback the teller said, “That was great except my friend was much more capable than you portrayed: he was, in fact, a lawyer”. This was a gross example of the dangers of playing the other. It highlights the risk that through stereotyping and presumption the idiosyncrasy of the other is appropriated and erased.

2004年5月在伊斯特本（Eastbourne）的一次会议上，面向代表们进行的一场一人一故事演出就体现了这些关切。在演绎一个关于一名男子带着残疾朋友乘火车旅行时遇到的问题的故事时，一名演员将那位残疾男子刻画得被动，并通过他的言语和动作暗示他可能还患有学习障碍。讲述者在反馈中表示：“表演很棒，只是我的朋友比你刻画的要能干得多：事实上，他是一名律师。”这正是扮演他者所带来危险的一个极端例子。它凸显了这样一种风险：通过刻板印象和武断推测，他者的独特性会被挪用并抹去。

We cannot escape ideology and culture; we therefore cannot rely on the personal experience of the actor as a source of incontestable ‘truth’. Lauren Love puts it this way: ‘…representation is inextricably embedded in dominant ideology’ (1995 p.276). She encourages actors to resist acting practices which trust in the ‘truth of the actor’s experience’ - the most well-known of these being, of course, the ‘Method’ and asks the question: ‘Whose experiences can reflect any truths other than those of the culture in which they were raised?’ ( p.277) From this position then, playback performers can do no other than reveal their cultural and ideological position in performances - the notion of the ‘universal performer’ is a fiction.

我们无法摆脱意识形态和文化的影响；因此，我们不能将演员的个人经历视为无可争议的“真理”来源。劳伦·洛夫（Lauren Love）如此表述：“……表演不可避免地植根于主流意识形态之中”（1995年，第276页）。她鼓励演员抵制那些依赖“演员体验的真实性”的表演实践——其中最著名的当然是“方法派”——并提出疑问：“除了自身成长的文化背景之外，还有谁的体验能够反映其他任何真理呢？”（第277页）基于这一立场，一人一故事表演者在演出中别无选择，只能揭示其文化与意识形态立场——“普世表演者”的概念纯属虚构。

Brecht takes a slightly different tack and warns that identification will mask the socio-political circumstances of the drama. He writes:

布莱希特则采取了略有不同的立场，并警告说，移情会掩盖戏剧中的社会政治背景。他写道：

> In order to produce A-effects the actor has to discard whatever means he has learnt of getting the audience to identify itself with the characters which he plays. Aiming not to put his audience into a trance, he must not go into a trance himself (….) Even if he plays a man possessed he must not seem to be possessed himself, *for how is the spectator to discover what possessed him if he does?* (1964 p.49) [my emphasis]
> 
> 为了产生A效应，演员必须摒弃他所学到的任何能让观众与自己所饰演的角色产生移情的手段。既然目的不是让观众陷入恍惚状态，他自己就绝不能陷入恍惚状态（……） 即使他饰演一个被附身的人，也不能显得自己也被附身了，*因为如果他自己也被附身了，观众又如何发现是什么附身了他呢？*（1964年，第49页）[强调为笔者所加]

In this sense identification risks a mystifying ‘naturalness’ potentially suggesting that the circumstances of the protagonist could be no other, that they are immutable. As Brecht writes in his prologue to The Exception and the Rule , ‘We ask you expressly to discover/That what happens all the time is not natural’ (1930 p.110). The processes of identification and empathy which are so revered in the ‘organic approach’ are rejected here because they either eradicate the idiosyncrasy of the other, or mask the socio-political forces at work in all identification.

从这个意义上说，移情存在制造神秘感、营造“自然感”的风险，这可能暗示主角的处境别无他法，是不可改变的。正如布莱希特在《例外与规则》的序言中所写：“我们明确要求你们发现／那些时常发生的事情并非自然”（1930年，第110页）。“有机方法”中备受推崇的身份认同与共情过程在此被拒绝，因为它们要么抹杀了他者的独特性，要么掩盖了所有身份认同过程中起作用的社会政治力量。

## Performance as a way of deeply sensing the other 表演作为深度感知他者的一种方式。

Should these concerns leave us pessimistic about the possibilities of representing the other in playback? Must we see it always as a form of colonisation or possession? These issues have been considered by Dwight Conquergood in relation to his practice as an ethnographer of performance - a researcher and performer of cultural practices. While recognising that performances cannot take place in ‘ideological innocence’ (1985 p.2), he wonders if they have the epistemological potential of ‘…deeply sensing the other’ (a hopeful possibility for playback practitioners). In the remainder of this article I want to introduce Conquergood’s mapping of different ‘performative stances toward the other’ and propose some ethical strategies that may reduce the dangers and worst excesses of ‘playing the other’.

这些担忧是否会让我们对在一人一故中呈现他者的可能性持悲观态度？我们是否必须始终将其视为一种殖民或占有形式？德怀特·康克古德（Dwight Conquergood）曾结合其作为表演人类学家——即文化实践的研究者与表演者——的实践，对这些问题进行了探讨。尽管他承认表演无法在“意识形态上的纯真”中进行（1985年，第2页），但他
仍思考表演是否具备“……深刻感知他者”的认识论潜力（这对一人一故事实践者而言是一种充满希望的可能性）。在本文的剩余部分，我将介绍康克古德对不同“针对他者的表演性立场”的梳理，并提出一些伦理策略，以期减少“扮演他者”所带来的危险及其最严重的弊端。

Conquergood identifies four performative stances toward the other which he considers ‘immoral’ (it is important, I think, that Conquergood is not afraid to use the unfashionable language of ethics here)

康克古德指出了四种针对他者的表演立场，他认为这些立场是“不道德的”（我认为，重要的是，康克古德在此并不惧于使用这种已不合时宜的伦理学语言）

1. **The custodian’s rip-off**: This is characterised by detachment and the plundering the experience of the other. In playback terms it could be considered as treating the stories of the other as trinkets to be collected and taken home. For Conquergood the immorality of such performances ‘can be compared to theft and rape’( p.6).In terms of playback theatre this could be a company descending on a marginalised community and boasting of the stories it uncovers.
**保管人的掠夺**：其特征是疏离，以及对他者体验的掠夺。用一人一故事剧场的术语来说，这相当于将他者的故事视为可以收集并带回家的廉价小玩意儿。对康克古德而言，此类表演的不道德性“可与盗窃和强奸相提并论”（第6页）。就一人一故事剧场而言，这可能表现为一个剧团突然闯入一个边缘化社群，并夸耀自己挖掘出的故事。

2. **The enthusiast’s infatuation**: This leads to facile and over-eager identification with the other. It assumes that goodwill and enthusiasm are sufficient. Conquergood quotes Tzvetan Todorov to illustrate his point:
**狂热者的迷恋**： 这会导致对“他者”的肤浅且过度热切的认同。它假定善意和热情就已足够。康克古德引用茨维坦·托多罗夫的话来阐明这一观点：

> ‘Can we really love someone if we know little or nothing of their identity, if we see, in place of that identity, a projection of ourselves or ideals’ (In Conquergood 1985 p.6)
> 
> “如果我们对某人的身份知之甚少或一无所知，如果我们看到的不是其真实身份，而是我们自身或理想的投射，我们真的能爱上那个人吗？”（引自康克古德 1985 年，第 6 页）
> 
The ‘enthusiast’ makes the assumption that their feelings of goodwill toward the other will overcome the problems of representing him. In playback terms it may be the belief that because I have experienced, say, bullying at school I can understand the systematic oppression of black people in the U.K. As Walter Benjamin argues, if we do so we will fail ‘…to touch the strangeness and the resistance of a reality genuinely different from our own’ (in Conquergood 1985 p.7). This position is, for Conquergood immoral because it trivialises the other.

这个“狂热者”假设，自己对对方的善意能够克服在呈现对方形象时所面临的问题。用一人一故事理论的术语来说，这可能表现为一种信念：例如，因为我曾在学校经历过欺凌，所以我就能理解英国黑人所遭受的系统性压迫。正如瓦尔特·本雅明所指出的，如果我们这样认为，我们将无法“……触及一种与我们自身截然不同的现实所具有的陌生感与抵抗力”（引自康克古德，1985年，第7页）。对康克古德而言，这种立场是不道德的，因为它将他者庸俗化。

3. **The curator’s exhibitionism**. Here Conquergood identifies the danger of making the culturally different exotic - of romanticizing the other in order to astonish the audience. He writes that:
**策展人的矫饰主义**。在此，康克古德指出了将文化异质性异国化——即为了令观众惊叹而将他者浪漫化——的危险。他写道：
> The manifest sin …is Sensationalism, and it is an immoral stance because it dehumanises the other. ( p.7)
> 
> 显而易见的罪过……是哗众取宠，这是一种不道德的立场，因为它使他者非人化。（第7页）

4. **The sceptic’s cop-out**. Finally the sceptic concludes that the chasm between the self and the other is so deep that there is no possibility of closing it. To Conquergood this is a morally reprehensible position because it ‘forecloses dialogue’.
**怀疑论者的推诿**。最后，怀疑论者得出结论：自我与他者之间的鸿沟如此之深，以至于根本不可能弥合。在康克古德看来，这种立场在道德上应受谴责，因为它“扼杀了对话”。

## Towards and ethics of playback theatre 走向一人一故事剧场的伦理

> One path to genuine understanding of others, and out of this moral morass and ethical minefield of performative plunder, superficial silliness, curiosity-seeking and nihilism, is dialogical performance…[T]he aim of dialogical performance is to bring self and other together so that they can question, debate and challenge one another. (Conquergood 1985 p.9)
>
> 通往真正理解他人的道路之一，也是走出表演性掠夺、肤浅的愚蠢、猎奇心理和虚无主义所构成的道德泥潭与伦理雷区的一条出路，便是对话式表演……对话式表演的目的是将自我与他者汇聚在一起，使他们能够相互提问、辩论和挑战。（康克古德 1985 年，第 9 页）
> 
> The teller is not asking that the actor get the story “right”, for this is impossible, and implicitly understood by the teller. Rather, they are asking that the actor meet the spirit with which they themselves told their story. *The actor’s attempt here is what is crucial - not the outcome.* (Penny 2002) [my emphasis]
>
> 讲述者并不要求演员将故事“演对”，因为这是不可能的，且讲述者对此心知肚明。相反，他们要求演员能够体现出自己讲述故事时所秉持的精神。*关键在于演员的尝试——而非结果。*（Penny 2002）[强调为笔者所加]

I agree with Bacon when he writes: ‘…never has a sense of the other seemed more crucial for our own humanity’. When there seems so much wilful misunderstanding of the other it may well be that playback theatre offers one of those forums where the self and other can be brought together: a space as Jonathan Fox puts it for ’radical social encounter (1995 p.4). There is no doubt in my mind that playback theatre can offer such an encounter largely because audience members dare to tell their stories and actors dare to embody them. However, as we have already seen, there is always a risk that we will eradicate the difference of the other in our performances - that well-intentioned empathy becomes oppressive colonization of the other.

我赞同培根的观点，他写道：“……对‘他者’的感知，似乎从未像现在这样对我们的‘人性’至关重要。”当人们对“他者”存在如此多的蓄意误解时，一人一故事剧场或许正提供了一个能够将“自我”与“他者”汇聚一堂的论坛：正如乔纳森·福克斯所言，这是一个实现“根本性社会相遇”的空间（1995，第4页）。我深信，一人一故事剧场之所以能促成这样的相遇，很大程度上是因为观众敢于讲述自己的故事，而演员敢于将其具身化。然而，正如我们已经看到的，在我们的表演中，总存在着抹杀“他者”差异的风险——那种出于善意的共情，可能会演变为对“他者”的压迫性殖民。

Before concluding I would like to offer some suggestions toward an ‘ethical disposition’ in relation to the other. It is my hope that this may begin a debate on how we might construct an ethics of playback theatre. I have deliberately chosen the word ‘disposition’ rather than ‘principle’ because it more accurately conveys the relational, active nature of the playback experience. I have drawn it from Richard Kearney for whom the question of ethics concerns the face-to-face ‘disposition’ toward the other. Ethics concerns how we are ‘disposed’ to the other, not a ‘position’ which is closed and non-relational, and as he writes:

在结尾之前，我想就与“他者”相关的“伦理态度”提出一些建议。我希望这能引发一场关于如何构建一人一故事剧场伦理的讨论。我特意选择“态度”一词而非“原则”，因为它更准确地传达了一人一故事剧场体验中那种关系性、主动性的本质。这一概念源自理查德·基尔尼，在他看来，伦理问题关乎面对“他者”时的“态度”。伦理关乎我们对“他者”的“态度”，而非一种封闭且非关系性的“立场”；正如他所写：

> It is clear that this notion of the ethical subject as a dis-position before the face of the other is radically social and political in its implications. (in Read 1993 p.91)
> 
> 显然，这种将伦理主体视为面对“他者”时的一种“态度”的观念，其内涵具有根本性的社会性和政治性。（引自Read 1993，第91页）

As playback performers we cannot escape from ideology or from our own partiality. However, in my view, this is morally acceptable as long as we don’t claim to be doing more that is humanly possible (for me, capturing essences comes into this category) and that we recognise our agency and accountability. Christian Penny’s statement that it is the ‘…actor’s attempt that is crucial - not the outcome’ seems to clearly express the point I am making. As a rule of thumb perhaps we should accept Anna Livia’s advice: ‘conjecture good, appropriation bad’ (1996).

作为一人一故事表演者，我们无法摆脱意识形态或自身的偏见。然而，在我看来，只要我们不声称自己做到了超出人类能力范围的事情（对我而言，捕捉本质就属于这一类），并且我们认识到自己的能动性和责任，这在道德上是可以接受的。克里斯蒂安·彭尼（Christian Penny）所言“……关键在于演员的尝试——而非结果”，似乎清晰地表达了我的观点。作为一条经验法则，或许我们应该采纳安娜·利维娅（Anna Livia）的建议：“揣摩是好的，挪用是坏的”（1996）。

One might ask why we need an ethics of playback theatre. Maybe we should do what comes ‘naturally’ to us. Maybe as we have sometimes said in Playback Theatre York: we just need to be ourselves. But, as we have seen, what is natural is ideology and anyway, as Richard Kearney has pointed out, ‘Ethics is against nature because it forbids the murderousness of my natural will to put my existence first’ (in Read 1993 p.94). I would concur with Alan Read when he writes,

有人可能会问，为什么我们需要一人一故事剧场的伦理学。也许我们应该做那些对我们来说“自然”的事情。也许正如我们在约克一人一故事剧场（Playback Theatre York）有时所说的那样：我们只需做自己。但正如我们所见，“自然”本身就是一种意识形态；况且，正如理查德·基尔尼（Richard Kearney）所指出的，“伦理学违背自然，因为它禁止了我那种将自身存在置于首位的、具有杀戮性的自然意志”（引自Read 1993，第94页）。我赞同艾伦·里德（Alan Read）的观点，当他写道：

> An ethics of performance is an essential feature of any philosophy and practice of theatre. Without it a set of cultural practices which derive from a very specific arrangement of power relations between people are unhinged from responsibility to those people. (Read 1993 p.6)
>
> 表演伦理是任何戏剧哲学与实践的本质特征。若缺乏这一要素，那些源于人际间特定权力关系安排的文化实践，便会脱离对相关人群的责任。（Read 1993，第6页）

What then might an ethical disposition toward playback performing look like? I would like to return the reader to the dilemma I posed at the opening of this article: am I able to play a teller whose cultural experience is significantly to my own? My thinking continues in the following way:

那么，针对一人一故事剧场的伦理态度应当是怎样的呢？我想请读者回过头来思考本文开头提出的两难困境：我能否扮演一位其文化经历与我自身截然不同的“讲述者”？我的思考如下：

> *If I was to improvise one of these tellers I would have little to go on except for cultural stereotypes which, given the tellers’ social position, would most likely be oppressive and insulting. So what am I to do? Well, of course, the first thing is that I would avoid the immediately obvious characterisations - just as I hope you would if you were to play a middle class English man! But then what? There is no point in denying difference: being black, from New York and into rap music, or being very sexually open is surely part of personal identity and to deny its importance would surely be a renunciation of a person’s identity.*
>
> *如果我要即兴扮演其中一位“讲述者”，除了文化刻板印象之外几乎无从着手；而鉴于这些“讲述者”的社会地位，这些刻板印象极有可能具有压迫性和侮辱性。那么我该怎么办？当然，首先我要避免那些显而易见的刻板刻画——正如我希望你在扮演一位英国中产阶级男性时也会这样做！但接下来呢？否认差异毫无意义：身为黑人、来自纽约且热衷说唱音乐，或者在性方面非常开放，无疑都是个人身份认同的一部分；否认其重要性，无疑就是对个人身份的背弃。*
> 
> *My conclusion is that the answer to my dilemma must lie in two directions. Firstly, I must acknowledge and, as best as I can, represent the experience of the teller. There will be much of his or her experience with which I will be able to identify. For the teller, as much as for me if the roles were reversed, it will be important that I make an attempt and that I show him that I have listened to and understood his story. But there will always be a gap - or perhaps a chasm - between my portrayal and his experience. So what I need to do, I think, is keep the gap between me as the ‘actor’ and me as the ‘character’ open and represented. In other words, I must allow the limitations of my acting to be visible.*
>
> *我的结论是，解决这一困境的答案必须从两个方向入手。首先，我必须承认并尽我所能地呈现讲述者的经历。其中会有许多我能感同身受的经历。对于讲述者而言——如果角色互换，对我来说也是如此——重要的是我做出努力，并向他表明我已经倾听并理解了他的故事。但我的演绎与他的亲身经历之间，总会存在一道鸿沟——或许甚至是一道深渊。因此，我认为我需要做的，是让作为“演员”的我与作为“角色”的我之间的鸿沟保持开放并得以呈现。换言之，我必须让表演的局限性显而易见。*

My first recommendation for an ethical playing of the other could therefore be expressed as:

因此，我对“以符合伦理的方式诠释他者”的首要建议可表述为：

## 1. Make the process of representation visible 让再现过程可见

If we, as playback actors, dispense with the idea of authentic acting then we need to make the gap between self and other visible onstage. Our responsibility is to convey that the enactment is not the authoritative version of the story - that it is work in progress, that it is one interpretation of many that are possible. We have a responsibility to expose the processes of representation at work in the performance. In other words we might accept, along with Brecht (1964) and then Boal (Boal 1979), that the theatrical illusion must be punctured so that the spectators are aware of the provisional and mutable nature of reality.

如果我们作为一人一故事演员摒弃“真实表演”的概念，那么就需要在舞台上将“自我”与“他者”之间的鸿沟呈现出来。我们的责任在于传达这样一个信息：即这场演绎并非故事的权威版本——它是一个未完成的作品，是众多可能诠释中的一种。我们有责任揭示表演中正在进行的再现过程。换言之，我们可以认同布莱希特（1964）以及随后波瓦（Boal 1979）的观点，即必须打破戏剧幻觉，使观众意识到现实的临时性和可变性。

To some extent I am arguing for the kind of acting that Brecht (1964) recommends in The Street Scene. The actor is not totally absorbed in empathic identification with the character but, to some extent, she is a ‘demonstrator’ who tells the story but maintains a visible gap between herself as performer and the character she is demonstrating. But how do we do this? These are some strategies that may help the performers ‘enact’ the inevitable gap between the actor and the character she is playing:

在某种程度上，我主张的正是布莱希特（1964）在《街景》中提倡的那种表演方式。演员并非完全沉浸于对角色的共情认同之中，而是在某种程度上扮演着“演示者”的角色——既讲述故事，又在表演者自身与所演示的角色之间保持着可见的距离。但我们该如何做到这一点呢？以下是一些可能有助于表演者“演绎”演员与其所饰演角色之间不可避免的距离的策略：

- use, like Brecht, the narrator role and so comment on the action from a rather more distanced position.
- 像布莱希特那样运用叙述者角色，从而从一个相对更超脱的立场对剧情进行评述。
- as I have seen occasionally in Playback Theatre York, the teller’s actor can briefly express - as a performer - the problems of representation she faces.
- 正如我在约克一人一故事剧场（Playback Theatre York）中偶尔所见，担任讲述者的演员可以以表演者的身份，简要表达她在表演中面临的问题。
- the performers may choose to work with an extended metaphor which allows exploration of the story but avoids the problems of naturalistic portrayal.
- 表演者可以选择运用一种延伸的隐喻，这既能探索故事，又能避免自然主义表演所带来的问题。
- the teller’s actor is careful not to give herself up to strong emotional identification.
- 讲述者演员需谨慎避免让自己陷入强烈的情感身份认同。

In addition readers may be interested in looking at the advice Susan Evans and William Layman gave after staging a playback performance for the families and friends of fire-fighters following a forest fire in Washington State in July 2001 (Evans and Layman 2001 p.7). It provides wise guidance on the sensitive conduct of a very delicate performance.

此外，读者或许会对苏珊·埃文斯（Susan Evans）和威廉·莱曼（William Layman）在2001年7月华盛顿州发生森林大火后，为消防员的家属和朋友举办一人一故事演出后所提出的建议感兴趣（埃文斯和莱曼，2001年，第7页）。这些建议就如何谨慎处理这场极其敏感的演出提供了明智的指导。

## 2. Vulnerability: body awareness and mortality 脆弱性：身体觉知与肉体凡胎

In the conversations of Playback Theatre York there is one piece of advice which, like the wisdom of the tribe, is regularly repeated. It is that when you are feeling vulnerable and uncertain of yourself you are most likely to give a good performance. The company recognises, as I am sure many playback practitioners do, that the vulnerability of the performer is one of the factors that permit lively and committed work. It seems that it is in the vulnerability and exposure of the body that a relational ethics may partly lie.

在约克一人一故事剧场的讨论中，有一条建议如同部落的智慧般被反复提及：当你感到脆弱且对自己缺乏信心时，反而最有可能呈现出精彩的表演。这个剧团认识到——我相信许多一人一故事实践者也是如此——表演者的脆弱性正是促成生动而投入的表演的关键因素之一。似乎，关系伦理在某种程度上正蕴藏于身体的脆弱与暴露之中。

Terry Eagleton argues, in his search for what he calls a ‘material morality’ that :

特里·伊格尔顿在探索他所称的“物质道德”时指出：

> ‘It is the mortal, fragile, suffering, ecstatic, needy, dependent, desirous, compassionate body which furnishes the basis of all moral thought’.
> 
> “正是这个肉体凡胎的、脆弱的、受苦的、狂喜的、需求的、依赖的、渴望的、富有同情心的身体，构成了所有道德思考的基础”。

I am not suggesting here that the body provides a final and authoritative source for an ethics of playback. It would be wise not to fall back into the trap of recreating an ‘essential’ body. However, the material, mortal body may be a useful trope to construct such an ethics.

我在此并非暗示身体是一人一故事伦理的最终且权威的来源。明智的做法是不要重蹈重构“本质”之身的覆辙。然而，物质的、肉体凡胎的身体或许是构建此类伦理的一个有用隐喻。

Terry Eagleton also argues for what he calls a ‘materialist morality’ located in the ‘moral body’ (2003 p.157). Taking Lear as his example, he writes:

特里·伊格尔顿也主张一种他所称的“唯物主义道德”，其根基在于“道德之身”（2003年，第157页）。他以李尔王为例写道：

> In the course of the drama, Lear will learn that it is preferable to be a modestly determinate ‘something’ than a vacuously global ‘all’…. [This is because] he is forced up against the brute recalcitrance of Nature, which reminds him pitilessly of what all absolute power is likely to forget, namely that he has a body. Nature terrorizes him into finally embracing his own finitude. (2003 p.182)
> 
> 在戏剧进程中，李尔王将领悟到，成为一个适度确定的“某物”，总比成为一个空洞的、包罗万象的“一切”要好…… [这是因为]他被迫直面自然的野蛮反抗，这种反抗无情地提醒他，所有绝对权力都可能遗忘的一点，即他拥有一个躯体。自然用恐惧迫使他最终接受自身的有限性。（2003年，第182页）

Relinquishing the ‘fantasy of disembodiment’, Lear finally comes to recognise that when his subjects told him, ‘I was everything; ’tis a lie - I am not ague-proof’ (Act 4 Scene 6). Eagleton goes on to argue that, not only does the recognition of mortality allow us ‘fellow-feeling’; it also acts as a brake to power. Wonderfully pithily he writes: ‘If power had a body, it would be forced to abdicate’. (2003 p.183)

放弃了“脱离肉体凡胎的幻想”后，李尔王终于意识到，当他的臣民对他说“我曾是万物；这不过是谎言——我并非免疫于疟疾”时（第四幕第六场），那确实是谎言。伊格尔顿接着论证道，对死亡的认知不仅让我们产生“同理心”，同时也对权力起到了制约作用。他以极具洞见的简洁笔触写道：“如果权力有躯体，它就不得不退位。”（2003年，第183页）

Fred Harris, a playback practitioner from the United States, proposes in a similar vein that, what he calls ‘mortal awareness’, is a key characteristic of effective playback performing. For Harris it is about being ‘…mindfully mortal: vulnerable to fate and destined to die, yet committed to life and its strivings.’ He suggests that a performer who possesses such awareness helps ‘us remember that our lives are limited and final, the actor helps us feel a renewed connection to life energy.’ (Harris 2002)

来自美国的一人一故事剧场实践者弗雷德·哈里斯（Fred Harris）也持类似观点，他提出所谓“对死亡的觉知”是有效一人一故事表演的关键特征。对哈里斯而言，这关乎“……有意识地面对死亡：既受命运摆布、注定一死，却又全身心投入生活及其奋斗。”他指出，具备这种意识的表演者能帮助“我们铭记生命是有限且终将结束的，演员帮助我们重新感受到与生命能量的联结。”（哈里斯 2002）

## 3. Sensitivity to and keen awareness of difference 对差异的敏感性与敏锐认知

In searching for a contemporary ethics and politics of playback beyond ‘the illusion of a universally binding ethic’, Jutta Heppekausen writes:

在探索超越“普遍约束性伦理的幻象”的当代一人一故事剧场伦理与政治时，尤塔·赫佩考森写道：

> Playback Theatre is one of those practices that enable the complex possibility of real contact, real meeting to take place between people. In this, it represents a form of moral learning…The concept of ‘morality’ has been released from the illusion of a universally binding ethic (the Enlightenment, Kant etc), which also served to avoid real personal closeness through standardised social codes of interaction….Moral behaviour today is not (any longer) something that can be regulated by convention. The meetings, which occur between people, happen *face to face*; there is an emotional relationship. The Playback Theatre art form has the potential to approach this area in some quite specific ways - but with the attendant risks.(2003 p.3) [my emphasis]
>
> 一人一故事剧场是那些能够促成人与人之间真正接触、真正相遇这一复杂可能性的实践之一。就此而言，它代表了一种道德学习的形式……“道德”的概念已摆脱了“具有普遍约束力的伦理”这一幻象（启蒙运动、康德等），这种幻象也曾通过标准化的社会互动准则来规避真实的个人亲密关系……当今的道德行为已不再是能够通过惯例加以规范的事物。人与人之间的相遇是*面对面*发生的；其中存在着情感纽带。一人一故事剧场这一艺术形式有潜力以一些相当具体的方式切入这一领域——但同时也伴随着相应的风险。（2003年，第3页）[强调为笔者所加]

She finds an ethics of playback in the acceptance and recognition of difference; the ‘opening of a dialogue’ through the recognition of the ‘other’; and the acceptance of ambiguity and uncertainty. Her approach to playback eschews organised or codified formulations of ethical positions, instead preferring to stress the ethics that are called for in relationships with others.

她认为，一人一故事剧场的伦理在于对差异的接纳与认可；通过承认“他者”而“开启对话”；以及对模糊性和不确定性的接纳。她对一人一故事的理解摒弃了有组织或成文的伦理立场表述，而是更倾向于强调人际关系中所需要的伦理。

This notion of the ‘face-to-face’ encounter as the ground of ethics comes from the work of the French philosopher, Emmanuel Lévinas (see Erickson 1999). Lévinas considered the other to be ‘unknowable’ and ungraspable. He suggests that this fact calls us to responsibility toward the other. Jon Erickson writes of Lévinas:

这种将“面对面”相遇视为伦理基础的观念源自法国哲学家埃马纽埃尔·列维纳斯（Emmanuel Lévinas）的著作（参见埃里克森 1999）。列维纳斯认为“他者”是“不可知”且无法把握的。他指出，这一事实呼吁我们对“他者”承担责任。乔恩·埃里克森在论及列维纳斯时写道：

> Lévinas continually states the importance of the maintenance of separation between self and the Other as essential for maintaining the ethical relation; in particular he marks the separation that prevents one from seeking reciprocity with the Other, which could as some point easily dissolve into an illusion of a complete identification between the two parties. (1999 p.10)
> 
> 列维纳斯一再强调，维持自我与“他者”之间的分离对于保持伦理关系至关重要；他特别指出了那种防止人们寻求与“他者”互惠的分离，因为这种互惠在某个时刻很容易演变为双方完全身份认同的幻觉。（1999年，第10页）

Over-easy identification with the Other (Llewelyn 1995) is, as we have already seen, one of the dangers of representing in playback theatre. Lévinas calls for a sustained awareness of the irreducible otherness of the other. I think this principle needs to be in the mind of performers as they approach playing the other. Putting this another way: one could say that their work will always ‘fail’ because there is no chance of accurate representation.

正如我们已经看到的，对“他者”的过度身份认同（Llewelyn 1995）是一人一故事剧场中角色再现的危险之一。列维纳斯呼吁我们持续意识到“他者”不可还原的异质性。我认为，表演者在诠释“他者”时，必须将这一原则铭记于心。换言之：可以说他们的表演注定会“失败”，因为准确再现“他者”根本无从谈起。

## 4. The key role of the ‘citizen actor’ and the ensemble “公民演员”和剧团的关键作用

Jonathan Fox asserts his belief in the ‘citizen actor’ who performs as needed and ‘melts back into the social fabric’, who ‘voluntarily absorb[s] the pain and problems of others’ and who offers ‘Service without security, without fanfare, without adulation’ (Fox 1994 p.214). This seems an important strategy that may permit performers to remain sensitive to difference. ensemble. Addionally, I would suggest that the robust life of a playback company is perhaps the best training ground for developing a heightened awareness to the otherness of the other.

乔纳森·福克斯坚信“公民演员”的存在——他们根据需要进行表演，随后“重新融入社会肌理”，他们“自愿承担他人的痛苦与问题”，并提供“不求保障、不事张扬、不求赞誉的服务”（福克斯 1994，第214页）。这似乎是一种重要的策略，可能使表演者始终对差异保持敏锐的感知。整体。此外，我认为，一人一故事剧场的充满活力运作，或许正是培养对“他者”之“他者性”高度敏感意识的最佳训练场。

## 5. Making the everyday extraordinary 让平凡变得非凡

Alan Read, in his consideration of the ethics of theatre, argues that theatre is ‘…a domain beyond everyday tyrannies to take better notice of the real pleasures of everyday life’ (1993 p.36). As Conquergood did, he warns of the dangers of sensationalising the life and practices of the other. In an argument, which I think we can adopt for playback theatre, Read says, ‘…the critical task might not be to domesticate the exotic but to exoticise the domestic’ (p.7)

艾伦·里德（Alan Read）在探讨戏剧伦理时指出，戏剧是“……一个超越日常暴政的领域，旨在更好地关注日常生活中的真实乐趣”（1993年，第36页）。正如康克古德所做的那样，他警告了将“他者”的生活与实践进行夸张渲染的危险。在一段我认为适用于一人一故事剧场的论述中，里德指出：“……关键的任务或许并非将异质的同化，而是将同化的异域化”（第7页）

In conclusion I have suggested that we need to question the notion of authentic acting since it is likely to mask the difference of the other. Instead I would suggest that all performance is a comprimise with the complexity of the other’s difference. My suggestions for an ethics of playback theatre in this regard are intended to continue a debate that becomes more relevant as playback theatre extends beyond its roots in western affluent societies.

总而言之，我提出我们需要对“真实表演”这一概念进行质疑，因为它很可能掩盖了“他者”的差异。相反，我认为所有表演都是对“他者”差异复杂性的妥协。就此，我针对一人一故事剧场伦理提出的建议，旨在延续一场辩论——随着一人一故事剧场逐渐超越其发源于西方富裕社会的根源，这场辩论正变得愈发重要。
