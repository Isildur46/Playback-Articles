# The Limits of Empathy: Avoiding Bias on Stage 共情的局限：避免舞台上的偏见

By Jonathan Fox (2006)

作者：乔纳森·福克斯

“I was working as a cab driver in NYC,” begins the white teller, as he sits down in the chair. “This black man gets into my cab late at night, and says he wants to go to Harlem.” My antennae jump out. Was the teller prejudiced? Was this going to be a story demeaning black people?

“当时我在纽约当出租车司机，”这位白人讲述者一边坐下，一边说道，“深夜时分，一位黑人男子上了我的车，说想去哈莱姆区。”我的警觉性顿时提高了。这位讲述者是否怀有偏见？这会不会是一个贬低黑人的故事？

What followed was a rather unclear story that skirted the line. As conductor, I made no special intervention, but I was uncomfortable with the way it turned out.

接下来讲述的故事相当模糊，游走于界限边缘。作为领航员，我没有进行特别干预，但故事的走向让我感到不适。

How do we deal in playback theatre with stories that reveal prejudice and bias is the subject of this article. It is not meant to be exhaustive in any way, but to sketch out a range of solutions. Many of you will have discovered interventions that I do not mention, and I look forward to hearing about them.

本文探讨的是：在一人一故事剧场中，我们该如何处理那些暴露偏见与歧视的故事。本文绝非旨在面面俱到，而是为了勾勒出一系列解决方案。你们中许多人可能已经发现了文中未提及的干预方式，我期待听到你们的分享。

First, let me state a principle: we will not objectify a minority on stage. This means that if a minority is portrayed as less than human in any way, we will do something about it.

首先，请允许我阐明一个原则：我们绝不在舞台上将少数群体“物化”。这意味着，如果少数群体以任何形式被描绘成“非人”，我们必须采取行动予以纠正。

When he heard this statement, a playbacker from the west coast said, “But I thought our job in playback was always to empathize with the teller, no matter what.”

听到这一表述时，一位来自西海岸的一人一故事剧场演员说道：“但我以为，无论发生什么，我们在一人一故事剧场中的职责始终是与讲述者产生共情。”

Playback theatre is not “just for the teller.” We must balance our empathy for individuals with our desire to reduce injustice and alienation.

一人一故事剧场并非“仅为讲述者而存在”。我们必须在对个体的共情与减少不公和疏离的愿望之间取得平衡。

This does not mean that we refuse to honor the stories of a dominant group. If many people feel threatened by immigrants, for example, and a (white) teller tells a story on this theme, that feeling is legitimate, and we can empathize with the teller. But at the same time, we do not want to dehumanize the immigrant, whose actions may be driven by a poverty and unrootedness beyond his or her control.

这并不意味着我们拒绝尊重主流群体的故事。例如，如果许多人因移民而感到威胁，而一位（白人）讲述者讲述了以此为主题的故事，这种感受是正当的，我们可以对讲述者表示共情。但与此同时，我们不希望将移民非人化——他们的行为可能是由自身无法控制的贫困和无根状态所驱动的。

What we do to counteract bias in a story is similar to interventions we might take in other special situations, where we are careful to humanize a character or object. A classic example might be a teenager telling about a fantastic party where there was a lot of dangerous drinking. While fully honoring the story, we might include onstage a bottle that speaks, giving some expression to the untold story of danger and self-destruction.

我们在故事中消除偏见的做法，类似于我们在其他特殊情境下可能采取的干预措施——即在这些情境中，我们会谨慎地将角色或事物“人性化”。一个经典的例子可能是：一位青少年讲述一场充满危险酗酒行为的狂欢派对。在充分尊重故事原意的同时，我们可以在舞台上加入一瓶会说话的酒，以此来表达那些未被言说的关于危险与自我毁灭的故事。

To be clear: I am not saying that you can never have a “bad guy” in a story. If someone tells a story about a boss that is overbearing, the actor can play the role play as very overbearing. A good story often has an antagonist. But in this case the antagonist is a powerful and privileged member of society. It is different when the antagonist is the weak and oppressed member of society.

需要明确的是：我并不是说故事中绝对不能出现“反派”。如果有人讲述一个专横跋扈的老板的故事，演员完全可以把这个角色演绎得极其专横。一个好的故事往往需要一个反派。但在这种情况下，反派是社会中权势显赫、享有特权的一员。而当反派是社会中弱势且受压迫的一员时，情况就截然不同了。

It’s time to give some specific examples and solutions.

现在是时候举一些具体的例子并提出解决方案了。

Story: At a university performance, a fraternity brother tells a “funny” story about him and his mates spying on a young woman whose bathroom window is visible from their building.

故事：在一场大学演出中，一名兄弟会成员讲了一个“搞笑”的故事，内容是他和伙伴们偷窥一名年轻女子——从他们所在的大楼可以看见那名女子浴室的窗户。

There are three kinds of solution. Two are conductor interventions, and one an actor intervention.

解决方案主要有三种。其中两种是领航员干预，一种是演员干预。

Conductor intervention #1: Conductor’s here and now reaction. There are many ways, verbal and nonverbal, to respond. For instance, the conductor, during the interview, can say, “that’s not very nice!” in a humorous way that does not alienate the teller but makes it clear to everyone in the audience that there is a questionable value system embedded in the story. If she wishes, the conductor might even decide to directly confront the teller, saying, “We will not enact a story that demeans or is at the expense of another.”

领航员干预 #1：领航员当下的即时反应。回应的方式多种多样，既有语言上的，也有非语言的。例如，领航员可以在讲述过程中以一种幽默的方式说：“这可不太好！”这样既不会让讲述者感到被排斥，又能让全场观众清楚地意识到，这个故事中蕴含着值得商榷的价值观。如果她愿意，领航员甚至可以直接质问讲述者，说：“我们不会演绎任何贬低他人或以他人为代价的故事。”

Conductor intervention #2: Next teller. The conductor does not intervene at the moment, but waits until the teller leaves the chair. In the example above, after the male’s story about laughing at a woman in a private moment, the conductor asks for the next story “from a woman,” trusting that it will constitute a kind of reply.

领航员干预 #2：下一位讲述者。领航员当时不进行干预，而是等到讲述者离开座位后再行动。在上述例子中，那位男性讲述了在私下嘲笑一位女性的故事后，领航员便邀请一位女性讲述下一个故事，相信这本身就是一种回应。

Actor intervention: Humanize the role. The actor chosen to be the woman needs to be bold enough to show her humanity in the enactment. For example, she might soliloquize as she gets ready for the shower that it is her one time to be by herself and collect herself for her day–something that will counterbalance the crude guffawing of the men who are watching, teller included. In such a situation, the actors playing the men must give this actor space. They must work together to honor the teller’s story, but also do something more.

演员干预：赋予角色人性。被选中扮演女性的演员需要足够大胆，在演绎中展现她的人性。例如，她在准备洗澡时可以自言自语，说这是她一天中唯一独处、整理思绪的时刻——这将与包括讲述者在内的男性观众那粗俗的哄笑形成鲜明对比。在这种情况下，饰演男性的演员必须给这位女演员留出空间。他们必须通力合作，既要尊重讲述者的故事，也要做更多。

Some companies have the skill to include unexpressed parts of a story in a collage format, either in the original enactment, or in a closing collage.

有些剧团擅长以拼贴的形式融入故事中未被表达的部分，无论是在最初的演绎中，还是在结尾的拼贴环节。

Another actor intervention, for very special cases, is to decline a role. In my view, this is OK. The conductor can support the actor and ask the teller to pick someone else.

在极特殊的情况下，另一种演员干预方式是拒绝扮演某个角色。在我看来，这是可以接受的。领航员可以支持该演员，并请讲述者另选他人。

If playback theatre teams know that these interventions are always available to them, then they can proceed to deal with a problematic story with confidence, always working together. The conductor will know that the actors can respond, and the actors know that the conductor can respond.

如果一人一故事剧场团队知道这些干预手段始终可用，那么他们就能充满信心地处理棘手的故事，始终保持团队协作。领航员会知道演员们能够做出回应，而演员们也知道领航员能够做出回应。

At the recent People of Color Working in Playback workshop that took place at the School, we reviewed these interventions, and another kind of situation emerged. It particularly concerns the case when the person picked to play the minority is a minority.

最近在该学校校举办的有色人种在一人一故事剧场中的工作坊里，我们回顾了这些干预措施，并发现了一种新的情况。这尤其涉及当被选中扮演少数族裔角色的人本身也是少数族裔时的情形。

In a story about a suspicious or scary person of color (POC), for example, the POC actor will often be picked to play the role. Knowing that he or she has the freedom–and the duty–to humanize that role can help that actor stay spontaneous.

例如，在一个关于可疑或令人恐惧的有色人种（POC）的故事中，通常会选定一位有色人种演员来扮演该角色。让该演员明白自己既有自由——也有责任——去赋予这个角色人性，有助于他或她保持自发的状态。

But there are some cases where it is too difficult, and the conductor should protect his or her actor who is a person of color from having to play the role at all.

但在某些情况下，这确实过于困难，此时领航员应当保护其有色人种演员，避免让他们不得不扮演该角色。

For instance, one of the stories told in a recent New Orleans performance as part of the Katrina Relief Project concerned a white teller who went into a hardware store in his northeastern US town to ask for donations. The owner was a grossly prejudiced individual who called the displaced people of the Gulf “knuckledusters.” In this case, the teller was not prejudiced, as proven by his volunteering in New Orleans to help clean up after the hurricanes. His story in fact concerned his dilemma of whether to challenge the owner on his views or to be quiet in hopes of obtaining much-needed materials.

例如，在最近作为“卡特里娜救灾项目”一部分于新奥尔良上演的一场演出中，有一个故事讲述了一位白人讲述者走进他所在的美国东北部小镇的一家五金店寻求捐赠。店主是一个充满偏见的人，他将墨西哥湾地区的流离失所者称为“铁拳”。在此案例中，这位讲述者本人并无偏见——他曾自愿前往新奥尔良协助飓风灾后清理工作，便足以证明这一点。事实上，他的故事聚焦于一个两难抉择：是该当面反驳店主的观点，还是保持沉默以期获得急需的物资。

In the event, the teller’s actor, who was a person of color, experienced her heart sink as soon as she heard that grossly insulting word describing African-Americans. She performed the story, and did it adequately, but she was upset about it, and the upset lingered. In a real sense she did not want to have anything to do with a story that contained an element so insulting to her.

当时，饰演这位讲述者的人（一位有色人种）一听到那个对非裔美国人极具侮辱性的词语，心头顿时一沉。她演绎了这个故事，表现也算到位，但她对此感到不安，这种不安久久未能消散。从某种意义上说，她根本不想接触任何包含如此侮辱性元素的故事。

Here is a case where the conductor, appreciating the actor’s discomfort, can choose to do the story using a short form, such as a chorus or a three-part story, in order to protect the actor.

在这个案例中，领航员若能体察到演员的不适，可以选择采用简短的形式（如大合唱或三段故事）来演绎这个故事，以此保护演员。

In the ongoing search for diversity that concern many companies, we need to be sensitive to the experience of our own persons of color in enacting stories that contain the stain of prejudice.

在许多剧团持续追求多样性的过程中，我们需要对剧团内有色人种演员在演绎带有偏见污点的故事时的感受保持敏感。

The white members also have the responsibility to play people of color in a nonstereotypical way and to fulfill the roles of prejudiced whites without “whitewashing” them.

白人演员也有责任以非刻板的方式饰演有色人种角色，并在扮演怀有偏见的白人角色时，避免对其进行“美化”。

From this it follows that a company that does not explore its own stories of issues of race, class, and other categories of oppression will probably not be sensitive to the stories embedding these themes when they occur in performances, and there is a strong likelihood that prejudice will be perpetuated. This process is never easy or quick. But it is essential.

由此可知，一个不探索自身关于种族、阶级及其他压迫类别故事的剧团，在演出中遇到包含这些主题的故事时，很可能无法对此保持敏感，而偏见极有可能因此得以延续。这一过程绝非易事，也绝非一蹴而就，但它至关重要。

Playback theatre is based on deep listening and empathy. But it is no less based on the idea that the story told, the story enacted, is good for everyone. That includes a minority person in the audience. That includes the performers, too.

一人一故事剧场基于深度倾听与共情。但它同样基于这样一个理念：被讲述的故事、被演绎的故事，对每个人都有益。这包括观众中的少数族裔，也包括表演者本身。

It should not be a choice between empathy or fairness. We need to raise our awareness and hone our skills so that we can do it all.

这不应是在共情与公平之间做选择。我们需要提高意识并磨练技能，以便能够兼顾二者。
