# What is Playback Theatre? 什么是一人一故事剧场

Jonathan Fox, M.A. and Heinrich Dauber, Ph.D.

The best way to describe playback theatre to someone who has never seen it is to paint a picture. Imagine a room with space at one end for a stage. On this stage sit the actors, facing the audience. On stage left sits a musician, surrounded by instruments. On stage right are two chairs. In one sits the “conductor,” who will function as a kind of master of ceremonies. The other will be taken by an audience member, who comes to it voluntarily and recounts a personal experience. The conductor asks certain questions, then when the interview is complete, turns the story over to the actors. Music plays for the transition. Then the actors enact the story, using mime, movement, spoken improvisation. Their aim is to capture the essence of the teller’s story. After suitable acknowledgment, the teller returns to the audience, and another teller comes forward.

要向从未看过一人一故事剧场的人描述它，最好的方式是描绘一幅画面。想象一个房间，一端留有空间作为舞台。演员们坐在这个舞台上，面向观众。舞台左侧坐着一位乐师，周围摆满乐器。舞台右侧有两把椅子。其中一张椅子上坐着领航员，他将担任一种主持人的角色。另一张椅子则由一位观众自愿坐下，讲述一段个人经历。领航员会提出一些问题，当访谈结束后，便将故事交由演员们演绎。过渡期间会演奏音乐。随后，演员们通过哑剧、肢体动作和即兴台词来演绎这个故事。他们的目标是捕捉讲述者故事的精髓。在适当的致谢之后，讲述者回到观众席，另一位讲述者走上台前。

A playback event consists of a series of stories told by audience tellers. It is a process totally spontaneous, made possible by adherence to the ritual. To the question of what happens if no one wishes to come forward, the answer is of course then there can be no playback theatre-but it almost never happens. For the fact is that when people sense an atmosphere of respect, as well as the actors’ skill, no matter what their background or culture, they show an eagerness to tell. It makes for compelling theatre. You get a chance to watch your neighbor’s story. Often it touches some vital aspect of your own.

一场一人一故事活动由观众讲述者讲述的一系列故事组成。这是一个完全自发的过程，正是因为遵循了这一仪式，才使得它成为可能。对于“如果没人愿意上台”这一疑问，答案当然是：那么就无法进行一人一故事剧场——但这种情况几乎从未发生过。事实上，当人们感受到尊重的氛围以及演员的专业素养时，无论其背景或文化如何，都会表现出讲述的热切愿望。这造就了引人入胜的戏剧体验。你有机会聆听邻座的故事，而这些故事往往会触及你自身经历中某些至关重要的方面。

Thus playback theatre is about stories (sometimes called scenes), by which we mean an experience of any sort recounted by the teller and dramatized by the actors. Playback performing companies also utilize a number of short forms, including the dance-like fluid sculptures and the dyadic pairs. In performance a playback company (actors, musician, conductor) will play for an audience, but playback theatre also often takes the form of a workshop, in which often a leader/conductor alone facilitates a group to act as well as tell.

因此，一人一故事剧场的核心在于自由演绎/故事（有时称为“场景”），即讲述者叙述的任何经历，由演员将其戏剧化呈现。一人一故事剧团还运用多种短形式，包括类似舞蹈的“流动塑像”和“一对对”。在演出中，一人一故事剧团（演员、乐师、领航员）会为观众表演，但一人一故事剧场也常以工作坊的形式呈现，通常由一位领航员/领航员独自引导小组进行表演和讲述。
