# Defining Theatre For The Nonscripted Domain¹ 在无剧本戏剧领域定义剧场¹

Jonathan Fox (1992)

乔纳森·福克斯 (1992)

THEATRE IS STRONGLY ASSOCIATED with a play in the popular mind—that is, a production which starts with a script written by a playwright. Yet there are many kinds of theatrical performance that do not make use of playwrights—a drama therapy session, for example, or a group-developed skit performed by adolescents, or the routines of a clown. The term drama has come to distinguish what might loosely be called nonscripted from scripted theatre; it is often used polemically, both by those who wish to separate themselves from the hierarchical, product-oriented literary culture and by those in the other camp who wish to keep at arm's length what seem to be authoritarian and unreverendly homespun improvisational approaches to performing. The distinctions between theatre and drama seem to depend significantly on concepts of specialization and context, and at any rate, have often not been clear. For example, Brian Way called his seminal book *Development through Drama* (Way, 1967), and Viola Spolin called hers *Improvisation for the Theatre* (Spolin, 1963), even though both focus on young actors, developmental process, and scriptless performance. The purpose of this article is to propose a definition of theatre that does not depend on play writing and which comprehends examples of oral as well as literary theatre. The discussion will demonstrate that the therapeutic uses of theatre, which are largely nonscripted, have as much claim to legitimacy as the literary theatre, in that they place a value on clients' role-playing with awareness, and indeed are part of an older tradition. In the discussion below, oral theatre, nonscripted theatre, and improvisational theatre all refer to dramatic production that does not depend on a written script.

在大众观念中，“剧场（theatre）”一词与“剧本（play）”有着密切的关联——即以剧作家撰写的剧本为起点进行制作的演出。然而，还有许多类型的剧场表演并不依赖剧作家——例如戏剧治疗（drama therapy）过程、由青少年集体创作并演出的短剧，或是小丑的表演环节。“戏剧（drama）”这一术语逐渐被用来区分那些可宽泛称为“无剧本剧场”与“有剧本剧场”的表演形式；它常被用作论战工具，既被那些希望与等级森严、以产品为导向的文学文化划清界限的人所采用，也被另一阵营中那些希望与看似专制且不敬地自发即兴的表演方式保持距离的人所采用。剧场与戏剧艺术之间的区别似乎在很大程度上取决于专业化与语境的概念，而且无论如何，这种区别往往并不清晰。例如，布莱恩·韦伊（Brian Way）将其开创性著作命名为《通过戏剧的发展（Development through Drama）》（Way, 1967），而维奥拉·斯波林（Viola Spolin）则将其著作命名为《剧场即兴表演（Improvisation for the Theatre）》（Spolin, 1963），尽管两者的关注点都是年轻演员、发展过程以及无剧本表演。本文旨在提出一种不依赖剧本创作、且既涵盖口述剧场又包含文学剧场的“剧场”定义。下文将论证，剧场在治疗中的应用——其大多属于无剧本形式——与文学剧场同样具有合法性，因为它们重视来访者在觉知状态下的角色扮演，而且实际上属于一种更悠久的传统。在下文的讨论中，“口述剧场”、“无剧本剧场”和“即兴剧场”均指不依赖书面剧本的戏剧演出。

## Preliminary enactment 初步演绎

In order to understand modern scriptless theatre, we must begin with its origins in prelitterary culture. The dramatic enactments of oral cultures—the all-night religious ceremonies, the healing dances, the vision quests—are characteristically trance-inducing as a means to secure a distinctively redressive outcome. It would appear that an irrational context stimulates trance; thus Indonesian performers stimulate themselves "beyond thought" (de Zoete, 1938); the Kung of the Kalahari sing in senseless syllables to propit the dancers into trance (Katz, 1982); and many cultures make use of a hieratic language not understood by the auditors (Jenkins, 1979). In such enactments, the performer becomes a healer, laying hands on the ring of spectators or exorcising illness through shamanic acts. These events are also likely to be highly communal and intimate in nature: the performers will be known personally by the audience, who are liable to know each other, and the line between performer and spectator is often easily crossed.

要理解现代无剧本剧场，我们必须从其源于前文字文化的起源说起。口述文化中的戏剧演绎——通宵达旦的宗教仪式、治愈之舞、寻梦之旅——其显著特征在于诱发恍惚状态，以此来确保一种独特的修复性结果。似乎是非理性的情境会激发恍惚状态；因此，印尼表演者会将自己激发至“超越思考（beyond thought）”的境界（de Zoete，1938）；卡拉哈里沙漠的孔族人会用毫无意义的音节歌唱，以引导舞者进入恍惚状态（Katz，1982）；许多文化还会使用一种听众无法理解的神圣语言（Jenkins，1979）。在这些表演中，表演者化身为治疗者，或向围观的观众群施以手按，或通过萨满仪式驱除疾病。此类活动往往具有高度的社群性和亲密性：表演者与观众彼此熟识，观众之间也可能相互认识，表演者与观众之间的界限往往很容易被跨越。

As children of literary culture, in contrast, we have grown up with a distinctly different model of theatre, one based on language, tightly organized into a written composition, of which the purpose is not redressive, but aesthetic. Furthermore, this theatre is a primarily urban phenomenon, performed by specialists who are not part of the same small community as the audience.

相比之下，作为文学文化的产物，我们成长于一种截然不同的剧场模式之中——这种模式以语言为基础，被严密组织成书面作品，其目的并非修复性，而是追求美学价值。此外，这种剧场主要是一种都市景观（urban phenomenon），由专业演员表演，而这些演员并不属于与观众相同的小型社群。

There have been many contemporary practitioners, however, who have rejected literary theatre and sought to recreate aspects of the prelitterary model of enactment. These actors and directors are following a path, perhaps, cleared earlier by visual artists, dancers, and composers (Picasso, Humphrey, Stravinsky) who found inspiration in "primitivist" tribal forms.² They hoave rejected many aspects of their literary heritage and the culture of modern play production. They have welcomed ecstasy; eschewed plays; returned to the outdoors; allowed nonactors on stage; upheld political and even personal transformation as goals. They have allied themselves with the fields of experimental theatre, educational theatre, community theatre, therapeutic theatre, and comic-satiric improvisation.

然而，许多当代实践者却摒弃了文学剧场，试图重现前文字表演模式的某些方面。这些演员和导演或许正沿着视觉艺术家、舞蹈家和作曲家（如毕加索、汉弗莱、斯特拉文斯基）早先开辟的道路前行，这些艺术家曾从“原始主义”的部落艺术形式中汲取灵感。²他们摒弃了文学传统和现代戏剧制作文化的诸多方面。他们拥抱狂喜；摒弃剧本；回归户外；允许非专业演员登台；并将政治乃至个人的转化视为目标。他们与实验戏剧、教育戏剧、社群戏剧、治疗性戏剧以及喜剧讽刺即兴表演等领域结成了联盟。

² According to Christopher Small (1977, p. 33), the Paris World Exhibition of 1889, which had such an impact on the modernist painters, also featured performances, providing performing arts with an opportunity to experience prelitterary music and dance. Artaud's interest in "primitivism" plays is well known (Artaud, 1958); Moreno, the founder of psychodrama, was also subject to Dada and Expressionist influences, advocated a dramatist, rather than literary approach (Fox, 1987), and there are undoubtably other seminal figures in the nonscripted theatre who were specifically inspired by prelitterary models. The intellectual history of modern nonscripted theatre remains underdeveloped.

² 据克里斯托弗·斯莫尔（Christopher Small，1977，第33页）所述，对现代主义画家产生深远影响的1889年巴黎世界博览会还设有表演环节，为表演艺术提供了体验前文字时期音乐与舞蹈的机会。阿尔托对“原始主义”戏剧的兴趣众所周知（阿尔托，1958）；心理剧的创始人莫雷诺同样受到达达主义和表现主义的影响，他主张采用戏剧化的方法而非文字化的方法（福克斯，1987），而且在无剧本剧场领域中，无疑还有其他开创性人物专门从前文字范式中汲取了灵感。现代无剧本剧场的思想史研究仍显薄弱。

Not surprisingly, in most cases, there has been a more or less creative blend of prelitterary and literary values in the fashioning of what might be called a postlitterary theatre. For example, many experimental theatre companies which began by improvisationally developing their own pieces ended by using playwrights; practitioners of educational and therapeutic theatre that reject scripts nevertheless prefer to base their objectives on cognitive assumptions rather than on trance-induction and ecstasy.

毫不奇怪，在大多数情况下，在塑造所谓“后文字戏剧”的过程中，前文字与文学价值都或多或少地进行了富有创造性的融合。例如，许多实验剧团起初通过即兴创作发展自己的作品，最终却开始启用剧作家；那些拒绝剧本的教育戏剧和治疗性戏剧实践者，尽管如此，仍更倾向于将目标建立在认知假设之上，而非催眠引导和狂喜之上。

Despite this mix of literary and prelitterary values, the general trend remains valid. Many practitioners have been creating alternatives to the literary/aesthetic model of theatre. The question remains: have they left theatre, or is it appropriate to redefine the term to embrace at least some of their nonscripted dramatic activities? I would like to pose here a definition that suggests the theatre begins before play production.

尽管存在这种文学与前文字价值的混合，总体趋势依然成立。许多实践者一直在创造替代文学/美学戏剧模式的方案。问题依然存在：他们是否已经离开了剧场，还是说有必要重新定义，以至少涵盖他们的一些无剧本戏剧活动？在此，我想提出一种定义，剧场始于剧目制作之前。

## Theatre and consciousness 剧场与意识

The psychologist Julian Jaynes (1976) draws on left-right brain research and his own reading of prehistory to arrive at a theory which he encapsulates in the title of his book, *The Origin of Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind*. Insofar as his theory attempts to identify the beginning of consciousness in human evolution, he is drawn into conclusions about prehistory which are far from proven. Nevertheless, his ideas hold interest. His theory was sparked by modern discoveries about the hemispheres of the brain. Basically, Jaynes suggests that in early civilization, the human brain was structured to favor right-brain functions, such as an intuitive, nonverbal, practical approach to "voices." It was a time when we would call hallucinatory "voices" real, and as individuals, relied on their "bicameral" perspective for guidance. The bicameral mindset, according to Jaynes, included induction, trance, a strong communal urge termed the "collective cognitive imperative," and "archaic authorization." (p. 324)

心理学家朱利安·杰恩斯（Julian Jaynes，1976）借鉴了关于左右脑的研究及其对史前时代的解读，提出了一种理论，并将其浓缩在其著作的标题中——《意识的起源：双脑思维的瓦解》。鉴于他的理论试图追溯人类进化中意识的起源，他得出的关于史前时代的结论远未得到证实。尽管如此，他的观点仍颇具启发性。他的理论源于现代关于大脑半球的研究发现。简而言之，杰恩斯认为，在早期文明中，人类大脑的结构更倾向于右脑功能，例如以直觉的、非语言的、务实的态度对待“声音”。那是一个我们将幻觉中的“声音”视为真实存在的时代，作为个体，人们依赖这种“双脑”视角来获得指引。据杰恩斯所述，双脑思维模式包括引导、恍惚状态、一种被称为“群体性认知命令”的强烈社群冲动，以及“古老授权”。（第324页）

But as left-brain functions, enabling analysis, language, objectivity, and linearity, developed, the right brain became less dominant, and in the process, "consciousness" was born. Consciousness involves narratizing, "always seeing our visceral selves as the main figures in the stories of our lives." (p. 63) A "space" for thinking becomes located inside our heads. The brain conceives of lexical metaphor, including the idea of "me." Language is a key element in the process towards left-brain dominance: "Each new stage of words literally creates new perceptions and attentions." (p. 132) Thus "subjective conscious mind is an analog of what is called the real world," enabling us to understand reality and make decisions. (p. 55)

但随着左脑功能的发育——这些功能促成了分析、语言、客观性和线性思维——右脑的主导地位逐渐减弱，在此过程中，“意识”应运而生。意识涉及叙事化，即“总是将我们本能的自我视为人生故事中的主角”。（第63页）一个用于思考的“空间”由此在我们的头脑中确立。大脑构想出了词汇隐喻，包括“我”这一概念。语言是通向左脑主导地位过程中的关键要素：“词汇的每个新阶段都字面意义上创造了新的感知和关注点。”（第132页）因此，“主观意识是所谓‘现实世界’的类比”，使我们能够理解现实并做出决策。（第55页）

This theory firmly connects the idea of consciousness with story: Prior to consciousness, Jaynes seems to be arguing, humans were simply in experience, responding to basic survival drives and beyond that, to the voices of divine authorization. After consciousness, the conception of an "analog I" enabled the early human to stand outside of as well as be part of the run of events because he or she could describe them. It meant the individual recombination of experience, and became a vital component of human life.³

这一理论将意识的概念与故事紧密联系在一起：杰恩斯似乎在论证，在意识出现之前，人类仅仅处于体验之中，响应基本的生存本能，除此之外，还响应神圣授权的声音。意识出现后，“模拟自我”的概念使早期人类能够既置身于事件的进程之外，又成为其中的一部分，因为他或她能够描述这些事件。这意味着对经验的个体性重组，并成为人类生活的重要组成部分。³

³ Paulo Freire's definition of "critical consciousness," developed in response to 20th-century conditions in Brazil, has interesting similarities to Jaynes's concept of consciousness. (Freire, 1973, p. 48) Of course, Augustine was a major figure in contemporary nonscripted theatre is a protege of Freire (Fox, 1979).

Oliver Sacks writes about Jimmy G., a patient with Korsakoff's Syndrome, which is characterized by a complete absence of narratizing consciousness. To Sacks, the 20th century neurologist, it seems truly as if his patient has no "soul"; so fundamental is his deficiency. "To be ourselves," Sacks concludes, "we must have ourselves—possess, if need be re-possess, our life stories. We must 'recollect' ourselves, recollect the inner capacity for narrative of ourselves." (Sacks, 1987, p. 111) In short, this capacity to see our life as a story represents a major step in human evolutionary growth. It is one of our most irresistible passions. It is at once a preserver of what is worth remembering from the past and a pointer to what can be in the future.⁴

奥利弗·萨克斯曾描述过一名名为吉米·G.的患者，他患有科萨科夫综合征，其特征是完全缺乏叙事意识。对这位20世纪的神经学家萨克斯而言，他的患者似乎真的没有“灵魂”；这种缺失是如此根本。“要成为我们自己，”萨克斯总结道，“我们必须拥有自己——拥有，必要时重新拥有，我们的人生故事。我们必须‘重拾’自我，重拾内心叙述自我的能力。”（萨克斯，1987，第111页）简而言之，这种将人生视为故事的能力，标志着人类进化发展中的重要一步。这是我们最难以抗拒的激情之一。它既保存了过去值得铭记的事物，又指明了未来的可能性。⁴

⁴ Jaynes articulated his theories about early human development after focusing on schizophrenics who "heard" voices and epileptics who had had their corpus callose severed. Sacks's patients, although with different conditions, point to comparable conclusions. Sacks finds that his patients, who cannot "narrate" in a variety of fanciful ways, nevertheless maintain quite wonderful qualities of contact with the concrete, of energy of music and drawing, of living and of certain remarkable mental skills. These qualities have associations with the "bicornual" mode of thought. Jaynes asserts that the "narrative comes primarily in the course of human historical development. (Sacks, 1987, p. 103)

⁴ 杰恩斯在关注那些“听到”声音的精神分裂症患者以及胼胝体被切断的癫痫患者后，阐述了他关于早期人类发展的理论。萨克斯的患者虽然病情不同，却得出了相似的结论。萨克斯发现，他的这些患者虽然无法以各种富于想象的方式进行“叙事”，却依然保持着与现实世界接触的非凡能力，以及在音乐、绘画、生活和某些非凡的心理技能方面展现出的活力。这些特质与“双角”思维模式有关。杰恩斯断言，“叙事主要是在人类历史发展的过程中产生的。（萨克斯，1987，第103页）

The importance of narratization in the development of consciousness has implications for the concept of spontaneity, which depends on skill at knowing the "story of the moment." Thus spontaneity, like storytelling, depends on consciousness. In this sense spontaneity—an individual response to an individually constructed moment—exists only in consciousness, even though many of its characteristics seem to come from the right rather than the left hemisphere of the brain. Actually, of course, spontaneity demands an integration of left and right functions and is essential to nonscripted performance.

叙事化在意识发展中的重要性，对“自发性”这一概念具有重要启示，因为自发性取决于对“当下的故事”的领悟能力。因此，自发性与讲故事一样，都依赖于意识。从这个意义上说，自发性——即对个人构建的当下时刻所作出的个体反应——仅存在于意识之中，尽管其许多特征似乎源自大脑右半球而非左半球。当然，实际上，自发性需要左右脑功能的整合，并且对即兴表演至关重要。

## The steps toward theatre 迈向戏剧的步骤

Diverse theories about the origins of theatre, such as play-acting, ecstatic enactment, and ritual, when fit in Jaynes's scheme, make a certain rough sense to me.

关于戏剧起源的各种理论，例如角色扮演、狂喜式演绎和仪式，当纳入杰恩斯的理论框架时，在我看来似乎有某种大致的道理。

It would seem from earliest times human beings engaged in play-acting to pretend, for example, much as other mammals do. Such play enables the learning of survival skills and makes possible a certain level of interactive contact between individuals of the species—communication, as Batson has pointed out, which is not without sophistication because in the "as-if" of playacting the actors are dealing with paradoxical meanings. However, they always remain themselves. (Batson, 1972, p. 189)

似乎从远古时代起，人类就通过扮演游戏来假装，例如，这与其他哺乳动物的行为颇为相似。这种游戏有助于学习生存技能，并使同种个体之间能够进行一定程度的互动接触——正如巴特森所指出的，这种交流并非缺乏复杂性，因为在扮演游戏的“假装”中，表演者正在处理悖论性的含义。然而，他们始终保持着本真。（巴特森，1972，第189页）

Ecstatic enactment, acting for the purpose of generating trance, consists of a dance at the simplest level, involving neither role nor language. Surviving examples are the twirling dervishes, the Balinese sword dances, and the Kung healing dances.

狂喜式演绎——即以诱发恍惚状态为目的的表演——在最简单的层面上表现为舞蹈，既不涉及角色扮演，也不涉及语言。现存的例子包括旋转的德鲁兹派修道士、巴厘岛的剑舞以及孔氏的治愈之舞。

Ritual enactment becomes a means of making contact with transpersonal forces. It is distinctly un spontaneous in nature, since it demands consciousness (although the conductor of the ritual, who stands apart to the extent that he is responsible for beginning and ending the ceremony, may be in a position which invites consciousness). There is a widely held sense that in ritual enactment the actors "represent" their roles rather than "portray" them. Thus an actor is the bision. The Lakota vision dances described by Black Elk would seem to be in this category. (Neihardt, 1972)

仪式性表演成为与超个人力量建立联系的手段。其本质上明显缺乏自发性，因为它要求保持意识（尽管仪式主持人——他保持一定距离，负责仪式的开始与结束——所处的地位可能更有利于保持意识）。人们普遍认为，在仪式表演中，表演者是“代表”其角色，而非“扮演”角色。因此，表演者即为“幻象”。黑麋鹿所描述的拉科塔族“幻象舞”似乎就属于这一类别。（尼哈特，1972）

Theatre involves three elements absent from the above. First is the portrayal of a story: It will have certain narrative characteristics, such as characters, objects, settings, events, crises. It will be a definitional experience, defined by the spectators' own story lore, and in turn contributing to the self-definition of each spectator. And it will offer counsel, wisdom, insight, ideas of value.

剧场包含上述内容所缺乏的三个要素。首先是故事的演绎：它将具备某些叙事特征，例如人物、道具、场景、事件和危机。这将是一种定义性的体验，由观众自身的叙事传统所界定，进而有助于每位观众的自我定义。此外，它还将提供建议、智慧、洞见以及有价值的理念。

Second, theatre consists of actors playing roles. The Rubicon is crossed once actors "portray," i.e. accept the awareness that they are playing roles which are distinct from themselves. Such awareness is concomitant with Jaynes's "analog I." It implies the presence of the fictive element and the ability to act "oneself" and "not oneself" at the same time.

其次，剧场由演员扮演角色构成。一旦演员开始“塑造”角色——即意识到自己所扮演的角色与自身截然不同——便跨越了卢比孔河。这种意识与杰恩斯（Jaynes）所说的“模拟自我”相伴而生。它意味着虚构元素的存在，以及同时扮演“自己”和“非自己”的能力。

Thirdly, theatre involves performance, which can be defined as acting with awareness. Performance is rooted in intentionality and takes place before witnesses. What distinguishes theatrical from everyday performance, or a dramaturgical perspective of ordinary behavior (Goffman, 1959), is primarily the fictive element. In theatrical performance, performer and witness share an understanding about the action that "this is pretend." Without the acceptance of this paradox, the dramatic fiction breaks down, and we are left either with "nontheatre" or deceit. Thus in performing theatre, we must communicate the frame of pretend around our actions, and around that, the fame of intentionality, an awesome—and exciting—task, the ultimate form of spontaneity test, and a tease to the playful genius of out intelligence.

第三，剧场涉及表演，其可定义为一种有自觉意识的演绎。表演植根于意图性，并在观众面前进行。剧场表演与日常表演，或从戏剧理论视角看普通行为（戈夫曼，1959）之间的区别，主要在于虚构性这一要素。在剧场表演中，表演者与观众对行动有着共同的理解，即“这是假装的”。如果不接受这一悖论，戏剧虚构便会崩塌，我们最终面对的要么是“非剧场”，要么是欺骗。因此，在进行剧场表演时，我们必须传达出围绕我们行为的“假装”框架，以及围绕这一框架的“有意性”框架——这是一项令人敬畏且激动人心的任务，是自发性终极考验，也是对我们智慧中那种玩味天赋的挑逗。

Fully defined, then, theatre is the performance of a story by actors in role.⁵

因此，完整定义是：剧场是演员通过扮演角色来演绎故事的演出。⁵

⁵ Of course, we must keep in mind that there never was, or could be, a neat separation in the development of a literary culture between "theatre" and what went before. Undoubtedly the process of development was gradual. Jaynes, of course, fills his account of evolution with assumptions about a role in the middle of the bicameral period, although he considered an actor's performances were plenty of occurrences of actors consciously narratizing. Assuming a space for thought, there has been a gradual transition, but not necessarily an alteration, just as there has been a gradual progression, but not necessarily a sudden one. In fact, Jaynes points to quite spontaneous qualities of mind on the part of modern human beings.

⁵ 当然，我们必须牢记，在文学文化的发展历程中，“剧场”与其前身之间从未存在、也绝不可能存在一种泾渭分明的界限。毫无疑问，这一发展过程是渐进的。杰恩斯（Jaynes）在其进化论述中，固然充斥着关于双脑时期中期某种角色的假设，尽管他认为演员的表演本身就是演员有意识地进行叙事化的诸多实例。假设存在思考的空间，那么虽然经历了渐进的转变，但未必是根本性的改变；正如虽然经历了渐进的发展，但未必是突如其来的。事实上，杰恩斯指出了现代人类思维中相当自发的特质。

The most important implication of this understanding of theatre for me is that it marks the genesis of theatre at consciousness, not plays. It helps explain the particular position of theatre in relation to the other arts. Dance, music, and the plastic arts are forms which, while they are created and enjoyed by conscious beings, nevertheless, do not demand consciousness. Wordless, they communicate through sound, movement, shape, color, texture and other elements which precede thought. In contrast, the nonverbal arts keep their feet in the timeless past, when language was not the be-all-and-end-all of communication. Writing, on the other hand, is divorced from this primal world of shape and sound. Experiencing a poem or a novel demands skills of reading, vocabulary acquisition, and literary comprehension which define it as a distinctively mental activity. We do not see the colors or the movements, we imagine them following the understanding of abstract letters on the page.⁶ Theatre is a bridge between the nonverbal and the literary arts. There is the potential for the most sophisticated linguistic expression, demanding education and thoughtfulness from the auditor, but at the same time the experience is sensory: the words will be spoken, by a personage standing there before you, and she may even be screaming. Theatre's involvement both with precocious and conscious communication is the source of much of its impact and arouses resistance in individuals who desire narrow frameworks.

对我而言，这种对剧场的理解所蕴含的最重要启示在于：它标志着剧场起源于意识，而非剧本。这有助于解释剧场相对于其他艺术的特殊地位。舞蹈、音乐和造型艺术虽然是由有意识的生物创造和欣赏的，但并不要求意识参与。它们没有语言，通过声音、动作、形状、色彩、质感以及其他先于思维的元素进行交流。相比之下，非语言艺术仍扎根于那个没有语言的永恒过去，那时语言还不是沟通的全部。另一方面，写作则脱离了这个由形状和声音构成的原始世界。体验一首诗或一部小说需要阅读技能、词汇积累和文学理解能力，这使其成为一种典型的精神活动。我们看不到色彩或动作，而是根据对页面上抽象文字的理解来想象它们。⁶剧场是无言艺术与文学艺术之间的桥梁。它既蕴含着最精妙的语言表达潜力——这要求观众具备相应的教育素养和思考能力——同时又是一种感官体验：台词将由站在你面前的角色亲口说出，她甚至可能在嘶吼。剧场既涉及早熟的、又涉及自觉的沟通，这正是其影响力的重要来源，同时也激起了那些渴望狭隘框架的个体的抵触情绪。

⁶ Writing that is read aloud and filtered to obsolescence falls into a different category from writing that is read by readers. Of course, the improvisational nature of the poet's challenge gave added dramatic impact to his recitation: we simply do not have the same concern over how or whether a reader will reinterpret the work—although how well it will be interpreted depends on the reader. Both the reciter and the reader, however, are not how theatrical their presentation, are not engaged in theatre for the reason that they are not in role.

⁶ 那些被大声朗读并因过时而被淘汰的文字，与被读者阅读的文字属于不同的类别。当然，诗人所面临的即兴创作挑战为其朗诵增添了额外的戏剧性效果：我们根本无需担心读者会如何或是否会重新诠释作品——尽管诠释得如何好，则取决于读者。然而，无论是朗诵者还是读者，其表现形式都不具备剧场性，他们参与其中并非出于剧场目的，因为他们并未扮演特定角色。

In considering, therefore, the "beginning" and "end" of the domain of theatre, there will thus be a significant area of theatrical expression existing between the manifestation of consciousness and literary play production. This is the zone of oral theatre. It includes Mummers plays, Topeng dramas, and contemporary nonscripted approaches from a Living Theatre piece to a psychodrama to a performance of the Mental Health Players. Each example needs to be examined in light of the definition. If there is performance, if the actors are in role, and if there is a comprehensible story, then it is theatre—even if there is no script, even if the purpose is to heal, even if the technology is crude and the community tiny.⁷

因此，在探讨剧场领域的“起点”与“终点”时，意识的展现与文学剧目创作之间，存在着一个重要的剧场表达领域。这就是口述剧场的领域。它包括木马剧、托彭戏剧，以及从“生活剧团”作品到心理剧，再到“心理健康剧团”演出的当代无剧本剧场形式。每个例子都需要根据这一定义进行考察。只要有表演，只要演员进入角色，只要有一个通俗易懂的故事，那就是剧场——即使没有剧本，即使目的是疗愈，即使技术粗糙且社群规模微小。⁷

⁷ For material on Mummers Plays and (oral) Medieval entertainments, see Brody (1970) and Waldrop (1969).


⁷ 关于“穆默斯剧”及（口述）中世纪娱乐活动的资料，参见布罗迪（1970）和沃尔德罗普（1969）。

On the other end, theatre, no matter how literary, must be fed by its prelitterary roots. If a theatre piece becomes too heavy with consciousness, its impact as theatre diminishes. One can assert in such a case that the ideas overwhelm the story, as is the case in certain poorly written plays. Or one can feel that a particular production is too lacking in elements of oral culture, such as spectacle, emotion, or an organic texture. Today's literary theatre involves such complexity of organization, so many distinct individuals with differentiated inputs, so elongated a period of preparation—in sum, such breakdown and bustle, that the production often loses its creative pulse. The effort in this century to imitate Stanislavski and others to help actors stay in touch with their subconscious can be interpreted as an effort to marshal right-brain powers to avoid succumbing to a juggernaut of theatrical left-brainedness.⁸

另一方面，剧场无论多么文学化，都必须汲取其前文字根源的养分。如果一部剧场作品过分沉溺于意识层面，其作为剧场的影响力就会减弱。在这种情况下，人们可以断言思想压倒了故事——正如某些写得糟糕的剧目所表现的那样。或者，人们可能会觉得某部特定作品过于缺乏口述文化的元素，例如视觉奇观、情感冲击或有机质感。当今的文学剧场涉及如此复杂的组织结构，汇聚了众多各具特色且贡献各异的个体，准备周期又如此漫长——总而言之，这种支离破碎与喧嚣纷扰，往往导致制作丧失了创造性的脉搏。本世纪为帮助演员保持与潜意识的联系而效仿斯坦尼斯拉夫斯基等人的努力，可被解读为一种调动右脑能力的尝试，以避免屈服于剧场界左脑主导的庞然大物。⁸

⁸ "Our subconscious gives us inspirations; yet we apparently can see this subconscious only through our consciousness, which kills it. Fortunately, there is a way out..." (Stanislavski, 1936, p. 13)

⁸ “我们的潜意识给予我们灵感；然而，我们似乎只能通过意识来观察这种潜意识，而意识却会扼杀它。幸运的是，还有一条出路……”（斯坦尼斯拉夫斯基，1936年，第13页）

In his theory of the transactional object, Winnicott emphasizes the importance of the "me-not-me" paradox—of the capacity of a normal infant to believe in the aliveness of its teddy bear and yet know it is not real. "Playing," he writes, "is inherently exciting and precarious." This characteristic derives not from instinctual arousal but from the precariousness that is subjective (near-hallucination) and that which is objectively perceived (actual, or shared reality)." Playing, Winnicott believes, leads to shared playing, which leads to cultural experience. And thence, we might add, to theatre. (Winnicott, 1971, p. 52)

在“交易性客体”理论中，温尼科特强调了“我-非我”悖论的重要性——即正常婴儿既能相信自己的泰迪熊是活的，又知道它并不真实。“游戏，”他写道，“本质上既令人兴奋又充满不确定性。” 这一特征并非源于本能的唤起，而是源于主观层面的不稳定性（近乎幻觉）与客观感知到的不稳定性（实际的，或共享的现实）。”温尼科特认为，“游戏”会导向“共享游戏”，进而形成文化体验。我们不妨补充说，由此又延伸至戏剧。（温尼科特，1971，第52页）

Performance can be regarded as a sophisticated form of play—play with a metadramatic frame calling attention to itself. My own experience suggests that mentally healthy three year-olds can do it with ease while, others, inhibited by social or developmental conditions, fail to accomplish various components of theatrical performance, including narratization, capacity for pretending, ability to play, ability to role play, awareness of engaging in these activities, willingness to engage in them in a social context (before witnesses), and management of the transitions between performance and nonperformance.⁹

表演可以被视为一种复杂的游戏形式——一种带有元戏剧框架、旨在引起观众注意的游戏。我个人的经验表明，心理健康的三岁儿童能够轻松做到这一点，而受社会或发育状况制约的儿童则无法完成剧场表演的各个组成部分，包括叙事化、假装能力、游戏能力、角色扮演能力、对参与这些活动的自觉意识、在社会情境中（在旁观者面前）参与这些活动的意愿，以及对表演与非表演之间过渡的管理。⁹

The challenge of creative manipulation of such paradoxical actions is just what drew Gregory Bateson, an early cybernetician, into the field of psychology. For Bateson, schizophrenics were individuals who could not move appropriately between domains of play and nonplay or distinguish between frames of meaning, while a fully functioning adult could easily maneuver in a world of constantly oscillating realities. In this sense human beings can be defined as *homo actos*—human as actor: well adjusted, always readjusting, able to perform or not perform appropriately. Such flexibility and creativity lies at the heart of Moreno's spontaneity theory (Fox, 1987); it is part of the "evolutionary step" (Bateson, 1972, p. 191) human beings have made towards increasingly complex and creative forms of action and awareness. As such, theatre is an appropriate vehicle for the diagnosis and treatment of individual and social problems.

对这类悖论性行为进行创造性驾驭的挑战，正是吸引早期控制论学者格雷戈里·贝特森投身心理学领域的动力。在贝特森看来，精神分裂症患者是那些无法在“游戏”与“非游戏”领域之间恰当转换，也无法区分不同意义框架的个体；而功能健全的成年人则能自如地在现实不断振荡的世界中游刃有余。从这个意义上说，人类可以被定义为*homo actos*——作为行为者的“人”：适应良好、时刻调整、能够适当地表演或不表演。这种灵活性和创造力正是莫雷诺自发性理论的核心（Fox, 1987）；这也是人类迈向日益复杂且富有创造性的行动与意识形式的“进化步骤”（贝特森，1972，第191页）的一部分。因此，剧场是诊断和治疗个人及社会问题的合适载体。

## Language in nonscripted theatre 无剧本剧场中的语言

What place does language play in theatre? It would appear that while language may not be essential to the portrayal of role and the narration of a story, it is of vital assistance to a theatre. Yet the language of a nonscripted production will be stylistically distinct from a play. It is important to understand how.

语言在剧场中扮演什么角色？表面看来，虽然语言对于角色塑造和故事叙述可能并非不可或缺，但它对剧场而言却是至关重要的辅助手段。然而，无剧本剧场的语言在风格上将与剧目有所不同。理解其中的差异至关重要。

First the compositional style will contain characteristics of oral composition. The text is likely to be created on the spot; according to Albert Lord, who did the pioneer research showing that the Homeric epics were orally composed, "the poem is not composed for but in performance." (Lord, 1965, p. 8) There is likely to be an emphasis on aggregation (as opposed to conciseness) and an acceptance of the improvisational moment (as opposed to a fixed text and set movements). To a literary ear and eye, the performance may seem underplanned and long-winded, full of repetitiousness and slow beats.

首先，其创作风格将包含口述创作的特征。文本很可能是在现场即兴创作的；据阿尔伯特·洛德（Albert Lord）所言——他曾进行过开创性研究，证明荷马史诗是口述创作的——“诗歌不是为了表演而创作的，而是在表演中创作的。”（洛德，1965，第8页）这种创作往往强调聚合（而非简洁），并接纳即兴的瞬间（而非固定的文本和既定的动作）。在文学性的耳朵和眼睛看来，这种表演可能显得准备不足且冗长拖沓，充斥着重复和缓慢的节奏。

Secondly, the meaning is conveyed according to different stylistic conventions. For instance, in the contemporary nonscripted theatre an intermediary (emcee, therapist, social worker, teacher) often coordinates verbal exchanges between audience and performances, taking suggestions for action, inviting comment afterwards, and so forth. The result is an attested transcript, in which the drama will be interlaced with social interaction.

其次，意义的传达遵循不同的风格惯例。例如，在当代无剧本剧场中，一位中介者（主持人、治疗师、社工、教师）通常会协调观众与表演者之间的语言交流，征集行动建议，并在演出后邀请观众评论等。其结果便是形成一份经核实的实录，其中戏剧内容将与社交互动交织在一起。

One consequence of this permeable line between art and non-art is a heightened sensitivity to context. The dramatic text that emerges will be informed by the social situation and is not independent of it.

艺术与非艺术之间这条界限的模糊性，其结果之一便是对语境敏感度的提升。由此产生的戏剧文本将受到社会情境的影响，而非独立于社会情境之外。

As an illustration, here is a part of the transcript of a performance of Playback Theatre. Playback Theatre is an approach based on the spontaneous enactment of personal story founded by myself and the original Playback Theatre company in 1975. While it is used in artistic and social development as well as therapeutic contexts, Playback Theatre is committed at all times to a redressive purpose. (Fox, 1982, 1991; Salas, 1983, 1992) On this occasion, the Playback actors had been enacting scenes, real stories of audience members dramatized in scene form using mime and spoken improvisations. Now they were lined up two by two enacting pairs, two feelings felt at the same time, again, as suggested by audience members. The intermediary, or Conductor, asks for another conflict:

作为例证，以下是一人一故事剧场一场演出的实录节选。一人一故事剧场是一种基于个人故事的自发演绎方法，由我与最初的一人一故事剧场创始剧团于1975年共同创立。尽管它被应用于艺术创作、社会发展以及治疗领域，但一人一故事剧场始终致力于实现一种修复性目的。（Fox, 1982, 1991; Salas, 1983, 1992）此次演出中，一人一故事演员们此前一直在演绎场景——将观众的真实故事通过哑剧和即兴对话以戏剧形式呈现。此刻，他们两人一列，排成两列，再次根据观众的提议，演绎同时涌现的两种情感。中介人（即“领航员”）又提出了一个冲突点：

In this example, the context shapes the script in a number of ways. First of all, the dramatic scene begins with a question and response exchange between intermediary and spectator. Secondly, when the unexpected begins to happen and the actor's trousers are pulled down, the actor "ad libs" in a way that restores congruence to the scene. In the literary theatre, such moments are memorable because of their rarity, while in the nonscripted theatre, the task of finding language to integrate the dramatic (fictive) and here-and-now (social) story is an ongoing, common-place task. Thirdly, the teller is in this case one of the performers, the company musician, and her offering a moment, usually the function of the audience, makes a statement that we have one communal identity, that no one needs to be excluded from this community of memory (the phrase is from Robert Bellah et al., 1985, p. 153). Most importantly, the performer-teller's story itself makes a statement to the group as a whole, for previously to this enactment, audience members had been invited to be actors in a scene. Her "embarrassed but touched" also refers to her reaction to their work.

在这个例子中，语境以多种方式塑造了剧本。首先，这一戏剧场景始于中介者与观众之间的一问一答。其次，当意外开始发生、演员的裤子被拉下时，演员通过“即兴发挥”使场景恢复了协调性。在文学剧场中，此类时刻因其稀有性而令人难忘；而在无剧本剧场中，寻找语言来融合戏剧性（虚构）与当下（社会）故事的任务，则是一项持续且司空见惯的任务。第三，在此情境中，讲述者正是表演者之一——剧团的乐师；她主动贡献一个瞬间（这通常是观众的职责），以此表明我们拥有共同的身份认同，即在这个记忆社群中，没有人需要被排除在外（该短语引自罗伯特·贝拉等人，1985年，第153页）。最重要的是，表演者-讲述者的故事本身向整个群体传达了一种信息，因为在此演绎之前，观众已被邀请成为场景中的演员。她所说的“既尴尬又感动”也指她对他们表演的反应。

This example may not seem particularly profound. Yet it points to oral theatre's dependence on context, and the multi-layered meanings of the improvisational moment. It can help us appreciate the significance of Black Elk's line, when after an enactment by the community of one of his visions, he comments that: "The experience was not a long one, but it had great meaning, because it made a picture of the relation between the people and the bison, and the power was in the meaning." (Neihardt, 1972, p. 174) At the same time, we can also appreciate Richard Schechner's despair at documenting this kind of event, when he writes about the 3,000-year-old Indian Agnicayana drama, which is still performed and has been filmed: "What I am saying is that no matter what textual documentation exists, we do not know what Agnicayana was." (Schechner, 1985, p. 59) How meaning is elucidated in improvisational drama, remains, in my view, not well understood. Turner has underscored the larger significance of tribal dramatic events with his concept of "social drama" (Turner, 1969), but his analysis does not address the semiotic in any detail. In nonscripted theatre, the "text" is created according to a process close to everyday communication, involving, it seems, a spontaneous blend of direct, indirect, and symbolic means, both linguistic and iconographic. For theatre, however, the meaning must be heightened by consciousness. The performers must create a pregnant, charged, (what Turner terms) "ergotropic" atmosphere. (Turner, 1981, p. 9)

这个例子看起来或许并不特别深刻。然而，它指出了口述剧场对语境的依赖，以及即兴时刻所蕴含的多重含义。这有助于我们领会黑麋鹿那句名言的深意——当社群成员演绎了他的一段幻象后，他评论道：“这段经历虽短暂，却意义深远，因为它勾勒出了人与野牛之间的关系，而力量正蕴藏于此意义之中。”（尼哈特，1972，第174页）与此同时，我们也能理解理查・谢克纳在记录此类事件时所感受到的绝望。当他谈及那部已有3000年历史、至今仍在上演且已被拍摄成影片的印第安阿格尼卡亚纳（Agnicayana）戏剧时，写道：“我的意思是，无论现存何种文字记载，我们都无从知晓阿格尼卡亚纳究竟是什么。”（谢克纳，1985，第59页）在我看来，即兴戏剧中意义是如何阐明的，这一问题至今尚未得到充分理解。特纳通过其“社会戏剧”概念（特纳，1969）强调了部落戏剧事件的更广泛意义，但他的分析并未详细涉及符号学层面。在无剧本剧场中，“文本”的生成过程与日常交流相近，似乎涉及直接、间接及象征手段——包括语言和图示手段——的自发融合。然而，对于剧场而言，意义必须通过意识加以强化。表演者必须营造一种意味深长、充满张力、（用特纳的话说）“能动性”的氛围。（特纳，1981，第9页）

## Conclusion 结论

Summing up, theatre can be defined as the performance of a story by actors in role. This definition includes, as well as literary theatre, an improvisation zone, in which the performers manifest consciousness—of their performative act, of their fictive roles, of their narrative art—even though they are not making use of a play. Acceptance of this zone as a valid domain of theatrical exploration might lead to fruitful developments in our understanding of modern theatre. First of all, nonscripted theatre can benefit from criticism. For the most part, it is ignored by critics, who are staunchly literary-minded. Moreover, nonscripted theatre needs attention from critics with some knowledge and appreciation of the distinct objectives of improvisational theatre production. Secondly, arts funding sources need to expand their guidelines to include nonscripted practitioners: at present without a strong ethnic make-up (in which case there may be a special category of funding), it is almost impossible (in the US) for a process-oriented ensemble to receive funding as a theatre company. Thirdly, there is need for research: to develop new methods for the documentation and analysis of oral theatre; to study the levels of context-text related communication as they become manifest in an improvisational performance; to examine the spontaneous creative process of the improvisational actor (the results might have usefulness far outside the field of theatrical studies). Finally, students of drama need educating on the distinctions between nonscripted and literary enactment; on the aesthetic vs. redressive purposes of theatre; on theatre's roots in the small community vs. its place in the modern city; and on that narrating, self-reflexive communication of meaning that defines theatre's boundaries.

总而言之，剧场可定义为演员通过角色演绎故事的过程。这一定义不仅涵盖文学剧场，还包含一个即兴创作领域——尽管演员并未使用剧本，但他们在该领域中展现出对表演行为、虚构角色及叙事艺术的自觉意识。若将这一领域视为剧场探索的有效范畴并予以认可，可能会促进我们对现代剧场理解的富有成果的发展。首先，无剧本剧场可以从批评中获益。在很大程度上，它被那些坚定秉持文学导向的评论家所忽视。此外，无剧本剧场需要那些对即兴剧场制作独特目标有所了解并能加以欣赏的评论家的关注。其次，艺术资助机构需要扩大其资助准则，将无剧本实践者纳入其中：目前，如果没有明显的族裔背景（这种情况下可能会有专门的资助类别），以过程为导向的剧团（在美国）几乎不可能作为剧场团体获得资助。第三，亟需开展相关研究：开发记录和分析口述剧场的新方法；研究即兴表演中显现的语境与文本相关沟通的层次；考察即兴演员的自发创作过程（研究成果可能在剧场研究领域之外具有广泛实用价值）。最后，戏剧专业的学生需要接受以下方面的教育：无剧本与文学性演绎之间的区别；剧场的美学目的与修复性目的；剧场植根于社群的传统与其在现代城市中的定位；以及那种界定剧场边界的、具有叙事性与自我反思性的意义传达。

Such criticism, research, and education will help restore a new legitimacy to the idea of theatre as therapy. Therapeutic theatre need not be compared to literary theatre, either as alternative or aberration, but rather appreciated as part of a prior tradition that emerged in the healing ceremonial rituals of ancient civilizations and has been in practice ever since. While it involves story, portrayal, and performance as does literary theatre, this tradition has its distinctive characteristics of language and purpose, including a fluid oscillation between fictive and everyday reality, communality, and a deep commitment to healing both individuals and society.

此类批评、研究和教育将有助于为“剧场作为治疗”这一理念重塑新的合法性。治疗性剧场无需与文学剧场相提并论——无论是作为替代品还是异类——而应被视为一种源远流长的传统，它起源于古代文明的疗愈性仪式，并自那时起一直延续至今。虽然它与文学剧场一样涉及故事、塑造和表演，但这一传统在语言和目的上具有鲜明的特征，包括在虚构与日常生活现实之间的流畅转换、集体性，以及对疗愈个人与社会的深切承诺。
