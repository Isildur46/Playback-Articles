# Playback Theatre as a Methodology for Social Change 一人一故事剧场作为社会变革的方法论

By Bev Hosking and Christian Penny 

贝芙·霍斯金和克里斯蒂安·彭尼

## What is Playback Theatre? 什么是一人一故事剧场？

Playback Theatre (PBT) is spontaneous improvised theatre created through a unique collaboration between performers and audience. Someone tells a story from their life, chooses actors to play the different roles, then watches as their story is immediately recreated and given artistic shape and coherence. The telling and acting of these stories becomes like a conversation that enables a community to get to know itself better.

一人一故事剧场（PBT）是一种通过表演者与观众之间独特的协作而产生的自发性戏剧。有人讲述自己生活中的故事，挑选演员扮演不同角色，然后看着自己的故事被当场重现，并赋予艺术形式与连贯性。这些故事的讲述与演绎宛如一场对话，使社群能够更好地认识自身。

A performing group usually consists of a conductor (the facilitator), actors and a musician. The whole performance is improvised - the actors, musician and conductor have practiced together and they follow a simple structure, but the content of what might emerge during a performance is unknown.

一个表演团体通常由一名领航员（即带领者）、演员和一名乐师组成。整个表演都是即兴的——演员、乐师和领航员虽然曾共同排练过，并遵循简单的结构，但表演过程中可能呈现的内容却是未知的。

Playback Theatre was created and developed in the US in 1975 by Jonathan Fox and a group of theatre artists. Currently individuals and groups in over 25 countries are practicing this form of improvisational theatre in their local communities - in public theatres and workshops, in community, educational and clinical settings.

一人一故事剧场由乔纳森·福克斯（Jonathan Fox）和一群戏剧艺术家于1975年在美国创立并发展起来。目前，超过25个国家的个人和团体正在各自的社群中实践这种即兴戏剧形式——无论是公共剧场、工作坊，还是社群、教育及临床场合。

## Playback Theatre in the development context - two projects 发展背景下的一人一故事剧场——两个项目

PBT has been learned and practiced extensively in the so-called developed countries and only recently have there been moves to explore whether this form of theatre might be a relevant community and social development methodology in so-called developing countries.

一人一故事剧场在所谓的发达国家已被广泛学习和实践，而直到最近，人们才开始探索这种戏剧形式是否可以成为所谓发展中国家的一种有效的社群和社会发展方法。

This paper refers to two such initiatives, one in India and one in Kiribati, and offers some reflections on this work.

本文以印度的一个项目和基里巴斯的另一个项目为例，并就相关工作提出一些思考。

These two PBT projects are different in many ways - in how the projects were conceived, the structure and support for the work, the composition of the groups, and their stated purposes not to mention the differences in the two cultures where the work took place. Detailed reports of this work have been completed for NZODA.

这两个参与式戏剧项目在许多方面存在差异——包括项目的构思方式、工作架构与支持体系、参与小组的构成以及其明确的目标，更不用说项目实施地两种文化之间的差异了。关于这项工作的详细报告已提交给新西兰发展署（NZODA）。

### India: 1998-2000 印度：1998-2000

In 1995 an International Women’s PBT Company was formed to attend the NGO Forum in Beijing. The aim was to with participatory theatre practitioners from other parts of the world, and to introduce PBT as a social change methodology.

1995年，一支国际妇女参与式戏剧团成立，旨在参加在北京举行的非政府组织论坛。其目标是与来自世界其他地区的参与式戏剧实践者交流，并推广参与式戏剧作为一种社会变革方法。

Good contact was made with activists from India who were already using theatre as a medium for community development and social change. They were searching for a theatrical means to bring out the social realities of a community in order to assist them to face their experiences and work towards more enabling solutions. They saw PBT as a form that could complement their street-theatre work.

该团与印度的活动家们建立了良好的联系，这些活动家早已将戏剧作为社群发展和社会变革的媒介。她们正在寻找一种戏剧手段，以揭示社群的社会现实，从而帮助社群成员
直面自身经历，并致力于寻求更具赋能性的解决方案。她们认为 PBT 是一种能够与她们的街头剧场工作相辅相成的形式。

Together with two women activists from Tamil Nadu, various possibilities were explored for supporting the interest of the women in India in training in the PBT form. A number of initiatives were taken, including an invitation to PBT consultants, Bev Hosking and Mary Good, to run some PBT training workshops in the south of India.

与来自泰米尔纳德邦的两名女性活动家共同探讨了多种可能性，以支持印度女性参与 PBT 形式的培训。为此采取了一系列举措，包括邀请 PBT 顾问贝芙·霍斯金（Bev Hosking）和玛丽·古德（Mary Good）在印度南部举办若干 PBT 培训工作坊。

Since January 1998 in the course of three visits, eight training workshops have been run for groups of social animators, street theatre practitioners and activists from throughout Tamil Nadu, and more recently Karnataka.

自1998年1月以来，通过三次访问，已为来自泰米尔纳德邦全境以及最近的卡纳塔克邦的社会动员者、街头剧场实践者和活动家群体举办了八期培训工作坊。

These workshop participants work in a range of settings focussing on the education and development of ‘dalits’, women in both rural and urban settings, child labour and bonded labour issues, and the welfare and rights of street children (with a particular emphasis on girls and young women).

这些工作坊的参与者在各种环境中开展工作，重点关注“达利特人”的教育与发展、城乡妇女、童工和债役劳工问题，以及流浪儿童的福利和权利（特别侧重于女童和年轻女性）。

Since the first workshops a high level of on-going involvement and commitment of the participants has been evident. The groups have continued to meet to practice and to develop their skills. They have also offered numerous performances with grass roots community groups and villages, often in very challenging circumstances. Alongside some quite extraordinary work, there continues to be a robust questioning of the relevance in PBT in the Indian context.

自首批工作坊以来，参与者始终展现出高度的持续参与度和投入度。各小组持续举行聚会，进行排练并提升技能。他们还与基层社群团体及村庄合作举办了众多演出，往往是在极其艰难的条件下进行的。在取得一些相当非凡的成果的同时，人们对 PBT 在印度语境下的适用性仍持强烈的质疑态度。

There are currently three groups working with PBT in Tamil Nadu and two groups in Karnataka.

目前，泰米尔纳德邦有三个团体在开展 PBT 工作，卡纳塔克邦有两个团体。

Early on this work was undertaken on a voluntary basis with some support from local PBT communities. However it was not feasible to continue on this basis, and in 1999 the NZODA Good Governance Programme approved funding to enable this PBT training to continue.

早期，这项工作是在当地 PBT 社群提供一定支持的情况下，以志愿者的形式开展的。然而，仅靠这种方式难以持续，因此1999年，新西兰发展署（NZODA）的“善治计划”批准了专项资金，以确保 PBT 培训能够继续进行。

### Kiribati: 1998-1999 基里巴斯：1998-1999

The starting point for this initiative was a UNIFEM (International) project “Traditional Media for Gender Communication”. PBT was suggested as a parallel methodology with shared values that could address the same questions and achieve similar goals.

该倡议的起点是联合国妇女发展基金（UNIFEM，国际）的“传统媒体促进性别沟通”项目。有人建议将 PBT 作为一种具有共同价值观的平行方法，既能探讨相同的问题，又能实现相似的目标。

PBT consultants, Bev Hosking and Christian Penny, were asked by the MSC for the ‘Women in Development’ Project in Kiribati, Margaret Mohamed, to pilot a grass-roots project using Playback Theatre. The aim was to enhance the status and participation of women in the community, through the telling and enactment of stories and the consequent raising of awareness within the community.

基里巴斯“妇女参与发展”项目的项目负责人玛格丽特·穆罕默德（Margaret Mohamed）邀请 PBT 顾问贝芙·霍斯金（Bev Hosking）和克里斯蒂安·彭尼（Christian Penny），利用一人一故事剧场开展一项基层试点项目。该项目旨在通过讲述和演绎故事，从而提高社群内的意识，进而提升妇女在社群中的地位和参与度。

The pilot was run in two stages - November 1998 and May 1999. The work with each community took place over six days in each stage.

该试点项目分两个阶段进行——1998年11月和1999年5月。每个阶段与各社群的合作均持续了六天。

The PBT consultants worked directly with members of two village communities in their local ‘mwaneaba’ - one ‘rural’ and one ‘urban’. The groups comprised housewives, gardeners, fishermen, labourers, adolescents, mothers and grandmothers most of whom had little formal education.

PBT 顾问们直接在两个村落社群的当地“姆瓦内阿巴”（mwaneaba）——一个“农村”社群，一个“城市”社群——与当地居民开展工作。参与者包括家庭主妇、园丁、渔民、劳工、青少年、母亲和祖母，其中大多数人几乎没有接受过正规教育。

Women and men participated in the workshops, playing together and telling their stories. In this process, enough trust was developed between the women and the men in the group so that some of the core stories and conflicts around gender emerged, both privately in the workshop sessions and then more publicly in the performances.

男女参与者共同参加了工作坊，一起游戏并讲述各自的故事。在这个过程中，小组内的男女之间建立了足够的信任，从而使一些围绕性别的问题的核心故事和冲突得以浮现——这些内容先是在工作坊环节中私下探讨，随后又在表演中更公开地呈现出来。

Women and men were able work closely together and build on the work of each other in the enactment of stories. Actors were chosen to play roles in their stories without reference to gender. This was a very powerful approach to facilitating a greater understanding of the world of the other.

在故事演绎过程中，男女能够紧密合作，并在彼此的工作基础上相互补充。演员在扮演故事中的角色时，不以性别为依据。这种做法极具影响力，有助于加深对彼此世界的理解。

New pictures and possibilities of relationships between women and men were beginning to be voiced and witnessed in the community.

社群中开始涌现并见证关于男女关系的新图景与新可能性。

The work had an effect on those directly involved in the training, the many who sat observing throughout, and also on those who witnessed the stories in the public performances.

这项工作不仅影响了直接参与培训的人员，也影响了全程旁观的众多观众，以及在公开表演中见证这些故事的观众。

The process of learning the basic skills of PBT in the training workshops and the public performances created many opportunities for a growing understanding and strengthening of the relationships between women and men. It enabled women’s voices to be heard, and made explicit the roles women play in the community. It provided a platform for the community to gain a deeper understanding and valuing of their contribution and promoted women in active and attractive leadership roles.

在培训工作坊中学习 PBT 基本技能以及公开演出的过程，为加深对男女关系的理解、加强这种关系创造了许多机会。它让女性的声音得以被倾听，并明确了女性在社群中扮演的角色。它为社群提供了一个平台，使社群能够更深入地理解和珍视女性的贡献，并推动女性担任积极且富有魅力的领导角色。

## Playback Theatre as a methodology 一人一故事剧场作为一种方法论

Playback Theatre is a methodology that enables an exploration of an issue or concern rather than delivering a message. It is not an approach that offers teaching or immediate solutions to a situation, and in this way PBT differs from other social action theatre approaches.

一人一故事剧场是一种旨在探索某个问题或关切的方法论，而非单纯传递信息。它并非一种旨在进行说教或为某种处境提供即时解决方案的方法，正因如此，一人一故事剧场与其他社会行动剧场方法有所不同。

With this methodology the possibility of directly changing someone’s attitude is unlikely, but there is a strong chance that a person will see a situation or someone else in a new way through hearing and seeing the experience of another. The telling and re-enactment of a story creates the opportunity for a new perspective on the meaning and implications of the story to be opened up and for new and different responses to emerge.

采用这种方法论，虽然直接改变某人态度的可能性不大，但人们很有可能通过聆听和观察他人的经历，以全新的视角看待某种处境或他人。故事的讲述与演绎，为从新视角解读故事的含义与影响创造了契机，并促使新的、不同的反应得以涌现。

With PBT the starting point is not an attempt to move a community towards something, but a working towards a comprehensive understanding and expression of what is occurring and the underlying social values. There is a mirroring effect, which brings the hidden values or questions to the surface and to people’s awareness. In this way, the community gets a richer and more complex view of its own nature. Any movement towards change is grounded in the current reality as expressed by the members of a community through the telling of their stories and it emerges from a change in perspective on the part of individuals or the community as a whole.

PBT 的出发点并非试图引导社群走向某种目标，而是致力于全面理解和表达正在发生的事情及其背后的社会价值观。这种“镜像效应”将隐藏的价值观或问题浮出水面，并使人们意识到它们。通过这种方式，社群对其自身本质有了更丰富、更复杂的认识。任何变革的动向都植根于社群成员通过讲述故事所表达的当下现实，并源于个体或整个社群视角的转变。

The work is organic and slow and it is only possible to work within the parameters of what the group will tolerate. The group will only bring forward the stories they feel willing to share. In this way there is an inherent self-regulating function. It is unlikely that the group will address themes beyond what is comfortable and purposeful. The indirect nature of PBT can be frustrating and challenging for those who feel an urgency to find quick solutions or to see immediate changes.

这一工作具有有机性且进展缓慢，只能在群体所能接受的范围内进行。群体只会提出他们愿意分享的故事。由此，该过程具有内在的自我调节功能。群体不太可能探讨超出其舒适区且无实际意义的主题。对于那些急于寻找快速解决方案或期望看到立竿见影变化的人来说，PBT 的间接性可能会令人沮丧且充满挑战。

PBT presents the challenge of working without judgement and of promoting understanding of others who may have different values and beliefs. It is an effective process for bringing out the points of view of different sub-groups in a community and encouraging people to speak and listen to each other. In this work differences can be told, seen and heard alongside one another.

PBT 面临的挑战在于不带评判地开展工作，并促进对那些可能持有不同价值观和信仰的他人的理解。这是一个有效的过程，能够激发社群中不同亚群体的观点，并鼓励人们相互倾听、畅所欲言。在此过程中，差异得以并存，被讲述、被看见、被倾听。

### Working with, not against 合作而非对抗

With PBT we are working towards a coherent sense of group and community while respecting current custom and not undermining it. We are not making assumptions that any of the present values are inadequate, instead we are working towards different questions or explorations chosen by the community.

通过 PBT，我们在尊重现有习俗、不破坏其基础的前提下，致力于构建一种连贯的群体与社群归属感。我们并不预设现有的价值观存在不足，而是致力于探索由社群自主选择的不同问题或方向。

It may be used to stimulate change, to celebrate achievements, to explore questions, to address conflict and at the same time it will always build connections and strengthen ties within the group and in this way enhance the sense of community.

该方法既可用于激发变革、庆祝成就、探索问题、化解冲突，同时也能持续构建联系并加强群体内部的纽带，从而增强社群归属感。

The PBT methodology offers the opportunity for an open forum that can sit alongside traditional structures and doesn’t seek to change or replace them. However, the stories and the themes brought forward in a PBT performance have the potential to inform traditional modes of decision-making

PBT 方法论提供了一个开放论坛的机会，它可以与传统结构并存，并不寻求改变或取代它们。然而，PBT 表演中呈现的故事和主题，有可能为传统的决策模式提供参考。

### The value of personal experience 个人经历的价值

PBT creates a forum for members of a community to tell of their own experience and it also creates both a purpose and a vehicle this for sharing.

PBT 为社群成员创造了一个讲述自身经历的论坛，同时也为这种分享提供了目的和载体。

There is an underlying belief that each person’s experience is of value and that it is important to create a place for everyone to be heard and seen. In any community there are people who are on the margins, there are stories that are not told or hidden, and while they remain untold, the value or respect that a community grants each member is limited to some degree.

其背后蕴含着这样一种信念：每个人的经历都具有价值，为每个人创造一个被倾听和被看见的空间至关重要。在任何社群中，都存在处于边缘地位的人群，有些故事未被讲述或被掩盖；只要这些故事仍未被诉说，社群赋予每位成员的价值或尊重就在某种程度上受到限制。

PBT has been created in so-called developed countries where there is a high valuing of the individual and it is a form based on the telling of personal stories. The question of cultural relevance does need to be taken into consideration, as encouraging the telling of personal stories may not always be appropriate. To date an exploration of this question has taken place as a key component of our PBT work in development contexts.

PBT 诞生于所谓“发达国家”，这些国家高度重视个人价值，而 PBT 正是基于讲述个人故事的一种形式。文化相关性问题确实需要加以考虑，因为鼓励讲述个人故事未必总是恰当的。迄今为止，对这一问题的探索已成为我们在发展中国家开展 PBT 工作的重要组成部分。

Some groups have questioned the emphasis on the ‘personal story’ as opposed to focussing on ‘real social problems’. There has also been an appreciation of PBT as a personal-political approach to empowering people. As the work with personal stories has developed, so has the awareness that there are social and cultural dimensions to every story. When these are listened to and reflected with respect, an individual and a community can become more aware of itself, and act more consciously towards living in accordance with its core values or revising these.

一些团体质疑这种对“个人故事”的强调，认为应将重点放在“真实的社会问题”上。同时，PBT 也被视为一种旨在赋权民众的个人-政治方法而受到认可。随着个人故事相关工作的深入开展，人们也日益意识到每个故事都蕴含着社会和文化维度。当这些故事被尊重地倾听并加以反思时，个人和社群就能更加清楚地认识自身，并更有意识地采取行动，以践行其核心价值观或对这些价值观进行调整。

## Conclusion 结论

PBT work is in the community and about the community. Every story that is told is an affirmation of the life lived in that particular culture.

PBT 工作扎根于社群，也关乎社群。每一个被讲述的故事，都是对特定文化中生活的肯定。

Each time a story is told by an individual and witnessed by the community, there has been a strong response - delight, recognition, hope, at times grief and sadness. Above all there has been a strengthening of the connections between people. The value of the ‘witnessing’ in PBT and the effect of this in breaking down isolation in a community has been experienced and acknowledged extensively.

每当一个人讲述故事并得到社群的见证时，总会引发强烈的反响——喜悦、认同、希望，有时还有悲痛与哀伤。最重要的是，人与人之间的联系因此得到了加强。PBT 中“见证”的价值及其在打破社群孤立方面的作用，已被广泛体验和认可。
